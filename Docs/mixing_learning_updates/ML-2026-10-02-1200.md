## Outcome

The two DAFx26 cards from the 09:00 search were deep-reviewed against their equations, tables, official demos and version metadata. The review narrows both claims.

For Giant-FFT group-delay manipulation, exact reconstruction is demonstrated only for an unchanged analysis/synthesis pass. It does not establish transparent reconstruction after segmentation or displacement. The paper's `8000–12000`-bin segment widths depend on FFT length, sample rate and padding, so they are not portable settings. The paper compares Giant FFT and windowed methods conceptually but supplies no measured STFT/spectral-delay baseline. The official demo exposes eight WAV players, but no code, asset licence or reproducibility manifest was found on the page; the paper's CC BY 4.0 licence must not be extended automatically to the demo audio.

For PTFR/TIV alignment, the published table does not support a universal best optimizer or monotonic benefit from `2-opt`. `argsort+2-opt` improves both vector columns but worsens the musical sequence-matrix score (`100.266 → 107.568`); `Hungarian+2-opt` improves both matrix columns but worsens the musical vector score (`7.622 → 7.891`). The circular-OT example remains one author-interpreted C-major-to-C-minor path, and the learned scale geometry is fitted to 106 internally constructed profiles with no held-out corpus. The official demo contains one alignment suite, one five-step interpolation comparison and one scale sweep, with 32 embedded audio players, but no linked code, dataset or asset licence. `arxiv:2609.31971v1` is the same paper/version family, not independent confirmation.

### SRC-ML-20261002-0900-01 — Giant FFT group delay, deep-review boundary

- **Stable ID:** `conference:dafx:2026:DAFx26_paper_02`.
- **Primary paper / demo:** https://www.dafx.de/paper-archive/2026/papers/DAFx26_paper_02.pdf and https://vud.org/giant-fft-group-delay/
- **Reading depth now:** `full_text_plus_official_demo_structure_and_asset_manifest`; demo audio was not downloaded or measured.
- **Exactness boundary:** equation (1) is inverted by cumulative summation in equation (3), and the paper reports floating-point recovery when no transformation is applied. Once group-delay segments are moved or unsegmented bins are optionally zeroed, this no-op null result no longer proves transparent output.
- **Parameter portability:** group delay is expressed in radians per bin and converted to seconds using `2πfs/N`; the meaning of a width in bins therefore changes with sample rate, zero-padding and file duration. The reported `8000–12000` bins are example-specific.
- **Comparison boundary:** the STFT/spectral-delay discussion is conceptual. No matched baseline, runtime/memory benchmark, artifact metric or listener comparison is reported.
- **Demo audit:** eight first-party WAV players are exposed: synthetic original/reordered, glockenspiel original/reordered, harpsichord original/incoherent/per-segment displacement, and one modulated result. Observed durations range from `7.8 s` to `30 s`; transformed examples do not all share the original duration, so whole-file loudness or preference comparisons require preregistered equal boundaries.
- **Licence boundary:** CC BY 4.0 is stated for the article. The demo page provides no visible code link or separate licence for audio assets; do not ingest, rehost or treat them as project training data without explicit rights verification.
- **Revised conclusion:** offline creative hypothesis only. A valid project test must compare the unchanged and displaced variants through the same analysis/synthesis path, freeze every segmentation parameter, express displacement in time rather than raw bins, and keep a raw source render only as a separate diagnostic.

- **Optimizer audit:** no method wins all four Table 1 columns. `argsort+2-opt` is best for the two vector columns (`4.350`, `2.276`) but is worse than plain argsort on the musical matrix (`107.568` versus `100.266`). `Hungarian+2-opt` is best for both matrix columns (`89.847`, `13.012`) but is worse than plain Hungarian on the musical vector (`7.891` versus `7.622`). - **Demo audit:** 32 embedded audio players cover seven alignment rows (aligned source, fixed target and mixdown), five interpolation positions for two methods, and one scale sweep. No code, dataset, revision, licence or evaluation form is linked on the page.
- **Version resolution:** the arXiv submission is an alternate public version of the DAFx work. It must be merged into the same card rather than counted as independent confirmation.
- **Revised conclusion:** retain only for harmonic/arrangement research. Distances are objective- and representation-specific and must not control timing, phase, gain, EQ, dynamics or mix quality.
