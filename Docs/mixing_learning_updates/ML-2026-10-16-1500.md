### SRC-ML-20261016-1500-01 — PEACE: Joint Embeddings of DSP Effects Code and Audio

- **canonical_id:** arxiv:2610.03405v1
- **title:** PEACE: Joint Embeddings of DSP Effects Code and Audio
- **publication_date:** 2026-10-02
- **venue/status:** ISMIR 2026 proceedings paper; arXiv v1; paper licence CC BY 4.0
- **url:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03405
- **reading_depth:** full_text
- **content_read:** abstract, method, dataset generation, architecture, experiments, results, limitations and conclusion
- **citation_check:** partial
- **correction_check:** no correction or later version found in the targeted 2026-10-16 search; absence is not confirmation

**Method.** PEACE aligns Faust DSP code and wet audio with SLAP, using AFx-Rep for audio and either T5 or BoxGraph for code. Training uses 21 Faust effects and randomly generated serial chains of length one to three. The reported corpus contains 200,000 training, 8,192 validation and 4,096 test pairs. Audio is 10 s at 48 kHz, normalized to −18 LUFS, and represented with mid/side log-mel features.

**Observed results.** Overall within-effect retrieval remains modest: roughly 16.8–18.1% R@1 and 44.3–45.2% R@10. Dynamics/nonlinear classes are substantially weaker: compressor R@1 2.5–2.6%, gate 0.1–0.3%, limiter 0.2–0.5%, and stereo width 0.2%. For 420 ordered two-effect chains, the from-scratch BoxGraph model reports 17.4% R@1 and 61.4% R@10; order-tolerant scores are higher. The out-of-distribution test is limited to reverb/RIR retrieval and cannot establish transfer to dynamics, nonlinear effects or stereo control.

**Limitations.** Random parameters and random source/preset pairing weaken ecological validity; source audio is not guaranteed dry; no user study is reported. An inactive gate/limiter/width operation can be unidentifiable from audio. Mid/side magnitude features cannot distinguish mirror-image left/right panning. Loudness normalization removes gain cues. The study covers a known 21-effect vocabulary and serial chains, not arbitrary production graphs.

**Bounded conclusion.** Use only to rank top-K topology hypotheses inside a known effect library. Do not treat retrieval as exact parameter recovery, gain/compression estimation, stereo-direction inference, or evidence that a chain improves a mix.

### SRC-ML-20261016-1500-02 — CTAG-FX

- **canonical_id:** arxiv:2610.08182v1
- **title:** CTAG-FX: Reinterpreting Synthesizer Parameter Spaces for Expressive Tone-Shaping Audio FX Design
- **publication_date:** 2026-10-06
- **venue/status:** preprint
- **url:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08182
- **demo:** https://taylor3527eg-hub.github.io/ctag-fx-platform-demo/
- **reading_depth:** full_text
- **content_read:** complete paper including processor design, retrieval/optimization context, evaluation, results, limitations and conclusion
- **citation_check:** partial
- **correction_check:** no correction or later version found in the targeted 2026-10-16 search; absence is not confirmation

**Method.** Seventy-eight synthesizer parameters are heuristically reinterpreted as a fixed tone-shaping processor: input trim, pre-EQ, tone stack, two drive stages/fuzz, post-EQ, noise, clean/processed/noise mix and output trim. The broader A&R-CTAG workflow starts from 67 configurations and optimizes with LAION-CLAP, but the reported processor comparison bypasses that retrieval stage: GPT-4o directly instantiates ten configurations for each of four prompts. Role-structured mappings are compared with thirty scrambled permutations per configuration on one held-out 10 s clean-guitar performance.

**Observed results.** Role-structured mappings yield more differentiated long-term spectra and nonlinear measures across the four prompts than scrambled mappings. The analysis uses RMS matching, spectral difference, harmonic energy, high-harmonic share and crest-factor reduction. Twenty-eight of 1,200 scrambled renders became non-finite and were excluded.

**Limitations.** The mapping is heuristic rather than uniquely identified, optimal, physically equivalent or perceptually calibrated. No listener study is reported. The evaluation covers one guitar performance, four prompts and ten configurations per prompt. It excludes delay, reverb and time-varying modulation. Strong fuzz was less sensitive to mapping. The internal stabilization is informed by BS.1770 but is not a complete loudness/true-peak implementation. Signal metrics and CLAP do not establish perceptual text correspondence or transfer across instruments and sections.

**Bounded conclusion.** Treat the processor only as a generator of frozen research candidates for guitar tone exploration. It is not a semantic preset, a validated production chain or an auto-apply rule.

## Knowledge Cards

1. **KC-ML-20261016-1500-01 — Retrieval is hypothesis generation.** Audio-to-code retrieval can rank candidate effect topologies; it does not identify a unique chain, exact settings or mix quality.
2. **KC-ML-20261016-1500-02 — Excitation bounds identifiability.** A processor that does not materially engage on the input may be impossible to infer; activity checks must accompany evaluation.
3. **KC-ML-20261016-1500-03 — M/S magnitude loses direction.** Mirror panning can produce the same mid/side spectra, so direction-sensitive stereo features are required for left/right decisions.
4. **KC-ML-20261016-1500-04 — Loudness normalization removes evidence.** A normalized embedding cannot support gain or compression-setting recovery without an unnormalized side channel.
5. **KC-ML-20261016-1500-05 — Reverb OOD is not general transfer.** Success on RIR retrieval does not validate out-of-distribution dynamics, nonlinear or stereo effects.
6. **KC-ML-20261016-1500-06 — Structured mapping is not semantic truth.** Prompt-distinct signal behaviour supports candidate generation, not optimal parameter semantics or listening preference.
7. **KC-ML-20261016-1500-07 — Non-finite renders require a hard gate.** Every generated configuration needs finite-sample, peak and silence checks before measurement or listening.
8. **KC-ML-20261016-1500-08 — One performance plus proxies is insufficient.** Signal metrics and CLAP on one guitar excerpt do not establish perceptual correspondence, section benefit or instrument generalization.
