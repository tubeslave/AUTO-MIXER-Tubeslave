### SRC-ML-20260930-1800-01 — objective metric domain dependence

- **Title:** *Objective Measures of Perceptual Audio Quality Reviewed: An Evaluation of Their Application Domain Dependence*
- **Stable IDs:** `doi:10.1109/TASLP.2021.3069302`; `arxiv:2110.11438`
- **Publication:** IEEE/ACM TASLP, published `2021-03-29`; discovered for this queue item `2026-09-30`
- **URLs:** https://doi.org/10.1109/TASLP.2021.3069302; https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.11438
- **Reading depth:** full relevant methods, aggregation, results, discussion and conclusion
- **Method:** correlations between objective measures and subjective scores from seven audio-coding and seven source-separation listening tests; correlations from development datasets were excluded from each measure's aggregate; Fisher-z aggregation and significance testing were used.
- **Bounded conclusion:** objective metrics are domain- and distortion-dependent. The best reviewed aggregate was the 2f-model (`rho=0.90` audio coding; `rho=0.86` source separation), but even that evidence does not make a metric a universal acceptance endpoint. The authors explicitly limit transfer to small impairments and note difficult modern distortions.
- **Relevance here:** ViSQOL is less coupled than PAQM when PAQM is the training loss, but it is not an impartial ground truth. Acceptance still needs explicit signal diagnostics and loudness-matched listening on project material.
- **Limitations:** older than the AEROMamba work; covers coding and source separation, not rock mixing; does not evaluate this PAQM implementation; citation follow-up is incomplete.

- **Reading depth:** configuration and relevant training/loss paths; not executed
- **Verified configuration:** `gamma_paqm: 1`, 11.025→44.1 kHz, four-second segments, batch size two.
- **Failure path:** non-finite generator loss skips the batch; any exception raised by the optimization/backward path is caught broadly and also skips the batch.
- **Accounting problem:** epoch averages divide by loop index `i + 1`, including skipped batches, while the code records neither accepted/rejected counts nor sample IDs. Consequently, logged means can be biased downward and failures cannot be checked for content-conditioned selection.

## Knowledge Cards

### KC-ML-20260930-1800-01 — significance is condition-specific

Pairwise evidence supports PAQM gains on MUSDB in the reported configurations, not a universal benefit. PianoEval contains both a significant loss against AEROMamba-HQ and several non-significant comparisons.

### KC-ML-20260930-1800-02 — independent does not mean universal

A metric not used in training is less coupled to optimization, but its transfer still depends on audio material and distortion family. Treat objective metrics as witnesses with declared roles, never as the only judge.

### KC-ML-20260930-1800-03 — skipped batches need a valid denominator

If invalid batches are skipped, report attempted, accepted and rejected counts; compute means over accepted observations; retain reason and sample identity for distribution checks. A denominator containing rejected batches corrupts the log even if the model continues training.

### KC-ML-20260930-1800-04 — repository licence text outranks metadata ambiguity

Conflicting root licence and package metadata block integration. Do not infer permission from a README badge, package classifier or public availability.

### RULE-ML-20260930-1500-01 — metric endpoint separation (revised)

- **Sources:** `SRC-ML-20260930-1500-01`, `SRC-ML-20260930-1800-01`
- **Apply when:** a learned or automatic processor is trained, tuned or selected with a perceptual metric.
- **Change:** preregister four roles: optimized metric, less-coupled perceptual metric, explicit signal diagnostics, and loudness-matched blind listening.
- **Setting range / number origin:** no DSP preset; cited rates and bitrates are source conditions only.

- **Confidence:** high for evaluation design; low for direct mixing transfer.

### RULE-ML-20260930-1500-03 — invalid-batch accountability (revised)

- **Sources:** `ARTIFACT-ML-20260930-1500-01`, `ARTIFACT-ML-20260930-1800-01`
- **Apply when:** differentiable audio losses can produce non-finite values or backward exceptions.
- **Change:** fail closed by default; if continuing is explicitly justified, log exception class, sample IDs, attempted/accepted/rejected counts and content strata; average only over accepted batches.

- **Confidence:** high for reproducibility; not a mixing preset.
