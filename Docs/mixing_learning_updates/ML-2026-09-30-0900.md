### SRC-ML-20260930-0900-01 — SAGE: Semantic Audio Generative Encoder

- **Stable ID:** `arXiv:2609.32755`; arXiv-issued DOI `10.48550/arXiv.2609.32755`
- **Publication:** preprint v1, submitted `2026-09-26`
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32755
- **Discovery date:** `2026-09-30`
- **Reading depth:** `selected_full_text_sections`; abstract, introduction, method §§2.1–2.3 and experimental setup §3.1 read. Remaining result tables, appendices, code/weights and full listening protocol remain queued.
- **Actually read:** the system operates on 44.1-kHz stereo complex STFTs, predicts the complex spectrum directly, uses a compact 105M-parameter SwinV2 VAE, and distils a frozen CLAP teacher into the latent only after a detached warm-up. Reconstruction combines complex-STFT, mel, mid/side, KL, semantic, adversarial and feature-matching terms. The authors state that omitting their sum/difference loss made side reconstruction about 12 dB too quiet in their ablation, which is evidence for that model/training setup, not a universal stereo-mixing constant.
- **Evaluation design read:** five held-out corpora, objective reconstruction/distributional metrics, nineteen probing tasks, and a MUSHRA listening test are reported. The setup includes MoisesDB mixtures and stems, but no controlled rock-mixing improvement test.
- **Limitations for this project:** preprint; training cost is reported as thousands of A100 GPU-hours; the study evaluates codec reconstruction/latent semantics rather than mix decisions; CLAP-shaped semantics cannot stand in for calibrated level, peak, phase, stereo or preference measurements; code and weights are linked but were not downloaded, executed or licence-audited in this run.
- **Status:** source card added; selected-section conclusions only; `citation_check:partial`.

## Knowledge Cards

### KC-ML-20260930-0900-01 — Stereo preservation needs an explicit objective

SAGE's reported side-energy ablation supports an implementation-scoped lesson: a reconstruction or learned-processing system can score well while damaging stereo information unless mid/side or equivalent channel-covariance behavior is measured explicitly. It does not justify a fixed side target for rock mixes.

### KC-ML-20260930-0900-02 — Representation metrics are not mix-quality meters

Semantic probes, distributional fidelity and reconstruction metrics answer different questions. None alone establishes section balance, transient quality, mono compatibility, loudness safety or listener preference. Keep explicit signal metrics and a loudness-matched listening test.

### KC-ML-20260930-0900-03 — Candidate selection changes reported generation quality

YuE2's abstract distinguishes a single-checkpoint result from best-of-eight selection. Any comparison that selects the best of several candidates must disclose the candidate count, selector and compute; do not compare it directly with an unselected baseline.

### KC-ML-20260930-0900-04 — Metadata cannot support technique extraction

The two video titles identify useful topics, but captions were unavailable. They therefore remain catalogue entries, not evidence for attack, release, ratio, gain reduction, loudness or mix-bus topology.
