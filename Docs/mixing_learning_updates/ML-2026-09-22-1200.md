## Outcome

The queued [spherical sliced-Wasserstein paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.22028) was completed equation by equation and its companion implementation was audited statically at revision [`27d6b799`](https://github.com/yluo1/SH-IRT/commit/27d6b799546e30ad51ae899aea30e37c17444077). Its lower reported SSW error is useful evidence for transporting an **aggregate simulated angular density**, but not independent evidence for better SRIR motion or reverb. The same SSW geometry defines the interpolant and its headline evaluation; the experiment integrates each simulated RIR over 150 ms and therefore discards reflection timing before comparison.

The implementation adds important protections — SOMS/PSD reconstruction and final unity normalization — but the public path is not fail-closed. `sh_pdf_check` explicitly omits non-negativity; matrix condition numbers are printed rather than gated; inverse-CDF routines return the last iterate without a convergence/failure flag; the `tst` files reviewed are plotting/performance scripts with no assertions. The optional exponential slice-weighting paths also require a regression test: transport writes a time-independent weight through linear indexing of an `N x T` matrix, while distance normalization applies both total-weight normalization and an additional division by the number of slices.

A targeted contradiction check found and fully read the independent preprint [Optimal transport of image sources for interpolation of room impulse responses with moving sources](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.17237v1), submitted 15 September 2026. It evaluates explicit time-domain RIR error and image-source assignment over 256 simulated trials, and shows that assignment improvements need not remove RIR-NMSE floors when visibility/fading is mismodelled. This strengthens the requirement for time-resolved and independent evaluation, while still providing no measured-room or listening validation.

### `SRC-ML-20260922-1200-01` — independent time-resolved comparator

- Source: Jesper Brunnström, Filip Elvander and Isabel Haasler, [Optimal transport of image sources for interpolation of room impulse responses with moving sources](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.17237v1), `arXiv:2609.17237v1`, 15 September 2026; preprint under the arXiv perpetual non-exclusive licence.
- Reading depth: `full_text_complete`.
- Method: partial optimal transport of detected image-source positions; source-informed and maximum-likelihood costs; dummy transport for unmatched reflections; explicit RIR NMSE and assignment error.
- Result: mean results over 256 simulated statistical/ISM trials favor partial OT over linear interpolation except at very high noise; on ISM data the gap to ordinary Euclidean assignment is smaller. When the shared-image-source proportion is low, improved assignment does not remove the RIR-NMSE floor because the fading model mismatches visibility changes.
- Limits: simulated image sources and bandlimited analytic RIR error (`B=250 Hz` in the reported trials); binary visibility/unit weights; no measured-room validation, full-band rendering, listening, localization or production-mix test.
