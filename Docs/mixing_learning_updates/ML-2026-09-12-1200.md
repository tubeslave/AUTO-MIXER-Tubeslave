**Update ID:** `ML-2026-09-12-1200`  
**Deep topic:** peak control, clipping/limiting, true peak and transient preservation in loud rock  
**State:** research only; `candidate / auto_apply:false`; all audio experiments `not_run`

## What is genuinely new

1. Peak reduction is better framed as a constrained optimisation problem: reduce peak excursion while limiting *perceptual detectability of the error*, rather than minimising crest factor alone. Jeannerot et al. reported a better objective loudness/quality trade-off than their compressor and clipper baselines, but only on 16 short kick samples, downsampled to 1 kHz, using PEAQ rather than a subjective listening test. This is a research direction, not a mastering preset.
2. A clipper followed by one or more limiters is a plausible way to distribute peak-control workload, but the video evidence is a single engineer demonstration, not a controlled loudness-matched comparison. Multiple nonlinear stages can also accumulate distortion.
3. Limiter lookahead and release behaviour expose a three-way compromise between transient retention/apparent loudness, distortion/aliasing, and pumping. The correct setting is programme-dependent.
4. Oversampling and true-peak limiting are related but distinct. Oversampling primarily reduces aliasing and inter-sample overshoot generated by fast nonlinear processing; true-peak limiting enforces a ceiling according to a particular estimator. One does not make the other redundant.
5. `-1 dBTP` is a useful delivery-safe choice in many workflows, but not a universal law. FabFilter explicitly states that no single output value is correct; it depends on the delivery specification and measured true peaks. This contradicts the categorical wording in one studied video.
6. Limiter comparisons are invalid if one variant simply works harder. Compare equal output constraints and loudness-matched renders; inspect both sample peak and true peak. For tiny sonic differences, use blind ABX or paired preference, not sighted plugin switching.

## Literature and official documentation actually read

### SRC-ML-20260912-1200-01 — Jeannerot et al. (ICASSP 2022)

- **Title:** *Increasing Loudness in Audio Signals: A Perceptually Motivated Approach to Preserve Audio Quality*
- **DOI:** `10.1109/ICASSP43922.2022.9747589`
- **arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.08183
- **Publication date:** 2022-05-23; **discovered:** 2026-09-12
- **Depth:** full five-page manuscript read, including method, psychoacoustic model, experiments, and conclusion.
- **Method:** minimise absolute peak value under a multitonal-masking detectability constraint, or minimise perceptual error under a peak constraint.
- **Evaluation:** 16 kick clips, maximum 1 s, downsampled to 1 kHz; comparison with a DRC, hard clipping and soft clipping; LUFS, crest-factor reduction and PEAQ used as metrics.
- **Important limitation:** no subjective listening test; authors explicitly say subjective validation is still needed. Generalisation to full-band 48 kHz rock masters is unproven.
- **Reproducibility note:** associated dataset DOI `10.5281/zenodo.5828375`; licence metadata could not be verified because Zenodo returned rate limiting, so it was not added as an approved audio source.

### SRC-ML-20260912-1200-02 — FabFilter Pro-L 2: Advanced settings

- **URL:** https://www.fabfilter.com/help/pro-l/using/advancedsettings
- **Depth:** complete official page read.
- **Verified details:** shorter lookahead can preserve transients and apparent loudness but increases possible distortion; less than 0.1 ms approximates hard clipping and can add aliasing/inter-sample peaks. Longer lookahead is safer but can soften transients. Attack/release and channel-link behaviour are programme-dependent.

### SRC-ML-20260912-1200-03 — FabFilter Pro-L 2: True peak limiting

- **URL:** https://www.fabfilter.com/help/pro-l/using/truepeaklimiting
- **Depth:** complete official page read.
- **Verified details:** true-peak limiting detects input true peaks and attenuates overshoot created by the limiting stage. FabFilter states that 4x oversampling plus at least 0.1 ms lookahead usually keeps inter-sample peaks within about 0.1 dB for this plugin; this number is implementation-specific.

### SRC-ML-20260912-1200-04 — FabFilter Pro-L 2: Oversampling and recommended workflow

- **URLs:** https://www.fabfilter.com/help/pro-l/using/oversampling and https://www.fabfilter.com/help/pro-l/using/recommendedworkflow
- **Depth:** complete official pages read.
- **Verified details:** 4x or 8x is recommended for normal use in Pro-L 2; higher factors are mainly practical for offline rendering. Oversampling can add very slight linear-phase pre-ringing and CPU cost. There is no single correct output ceiling; the delivery standard and true-peak measurement decide it.

## Candidate rules

All rules below are `auto_apply:false`.

### R-ML-20260912-1200-01 — distributed_peak_control_if_single_stage_fails

- **When:** final limiter shows audible pumping, transient collapse or distortion at the required delivery loudness.
- **Change:** test smaller peak-control contributions at source/bus/master rather than automatically adding another limiter.
- **Do not damage:** kick/snare impact, cymbal clarity, bass pitch definition, vocal consonants, stereo image.
- **Verify:** loudness-matched blind comparison; sample peak, dBTP, short-term LUFS, crest/transient shape, oversampled spectrum/null.
- **Undo:** if cumulative distortion, dullness or section imbalance increases.
- **Confidence:** medium.

### R-ML-20260912-1200-02 — limiter_time_constants_by_artifact

- **When:** choosing limiter style/lookahead/release for a rock master or drum bus.
- **Change:** audition settings along the distortion ↔ transient-softening/pumping trade-off; do not infer quality from plugin labels.
- **Verify:** chorus and sparse-section A/B at equal loudness and equal ceiling.
- **Undo:** if the result loses punch, creates low-frequency modulation, or adds harsh non-harmonic components.
- **Confidence:** medium-high.

### R-ML-20260912-1200-03 — oversample_nonlinear_peak_control

- **When:** offline rendering with fast limiting/clipping, especially at high loudness.
- **Change:** compare native, 4x and 8x processing; use higher factors only if the measured/listened result improves.
- **Do not damage:** leading-edge timing, brightness, CPU/latency budget in live mode.
- **Verify:** alias components, dBTP after render, level-matched listening.
- **Undo:** if pre-ringing/transient softening or instability outweighs the benefit.
- **Confidence:** high for Pro-L 2; medium for other algorithms.

### R-ML-20260912-1200-04 — delivery_specific_true_peak

- **When:** exporting a master.
- **Change:** set ceiling and true-peak mode from the named destination/specification; run a post-codec decode measurement when relevant.
- **Do not damage:** loudness/punch merely to chase an arbitrary global ceiling.
- **Verify:** output PCM and decoded delivery format.
- **Undo:** if specification changes or codec test shows unnecessary margin.
- **Confidence:** high.

### R-ML-20260912-1200-05 — source_peak_fix_before_master

- **When:** one identifiable source repeatedly drives master gain reduction.
- **Change:** test clip gain, envelope, source/bus compression or clipping on that source only.
- **Do not damage:** groove, tonal balance, drum identity and section role.
- **Verify:** source solo only for diagnosis; accept/reject in full mix and mono.
- **Undo:** if the source loses priority or the master does not improve.
- **Confidence:** medium-high.

### R-ML-20260912-1200-06 — perceptual_metric_is_advisory

- **When:** optimising crest/peak reduction automatically.
- **Change:** combine technical metrics with perceptual-error estimates and human listening; never accept a lower crest value by itself.
- **Do not damage:** transient fidelity, groove, timbre and emotional density.
- **Verify:** loudness-matched A/B plus Dmitry rating; metric must predict preference across multiple songs/sections.
- **Undo:** if metric improvement fails to predict listener preference.
- **Confidence:** medium.

## Handoff to mixing agents

Do not optimise loudness with crest factor alone. Treat clipper→limiter chains, lookahead and oversampling as programme-dependent candidates. Match loudness and processing depth before comparison. Set true-peak ceiling from the delivery path, then check the decoded format. Keep every new rule advisory until `EXP-ML-20260912-1200-01..04` is run and rated by Dmitry.
