## What is genuinely new

The shared base already named ITO-Master and SonicMaster as related work, so this update does not present them as newly discovered titles. It upgrades both to full source cards. The fresh part is the complete reading of **SonicMaster v5 dated 2026-09-11**, including its expanded long-form results and limitations.

The central result is a useful safety distinction for Automixer: **reference similarity, technical/production quality and listener preference are three different endpoints**. In ITO-Master, optimizing the reference embedding improved the selected Audio Feature loss and DRV, while several independent FAD variants worsened relative to the same model before ITO. An optimizer can therefore become more faithful to its objective without becoming unambiguously better.

## Sources actually read

### ITO-Master

Full HTML read: introduction, differentiable 46-parameter mastering chain, data, metrics, objective results, listening test and conclusion. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.16889) · [official code](https://github.com/SonyResearch/ITO-Master)

Bounded findings:

- The white-box chain exposes EQ, distortion, three-band compression, makeup gain, stereo imaging and limiting. Visibility helps inspection but does not make the optimization objective safe.
- ITO on the reference embedding improved the optimized AF loss and DRV. Several independent FAD scores became worse, so early stopping must watch held-out quality gates rather than the training objective alone.
- Direct optimization of the processor parameters with the content-independent loss performed poorly in this style-transfer setting.
- The MUSHRA-type study used 10 production-aware listeners, eight 30-second questions, a different-content reference and no hidden high anchor. It tested similarity to reference effects, not full delivery readiness.
- The single 11.8-second instrumental-rock text-prompt example is qualitative. Its “metal” direction is not evidence for a universal metal EQ curve, crest factor or LUFS target.
- The authors explicitly leave production quality and usability for future work and warn that an unsuitable reference can be matched well.

The official implementation was inspected at README/license depth. It is **CC BY-NC 4.0** and documents CUDA-oriented examples; no code, weights, audio or compute job was run.

### SonicMaster v5

Full HTML read: data construction, architecture, metrics, short- and long-form results, two listening tests, degradation table and full limitations. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.03448) · [official code](https://github.com/AMAAI-Lab/SonicMaster)

Bounded findings:

- The training set contains about 25k selected 30-second Jamendo segments and 175k clean/degraded pairs with one to three synthetic degradations drawn from 19 functions across EQ, dynamics, reverb, amplitude and stereo.
- It is a controlled inverse-degradation benchmark, not a corpus of professional raw-to-master rock sessions. The large compressor/EQ/transient-shaper ranges in its table create corrupted examples; they are **not mastering presets**.
- Compression, punch, clipping and stereo are evaluated with task-specific proxies such as frame-RMS spread, onset strength, spectral flatness and mid/side energy ratio. These proxy scores do not prove restored musical punch, unclipped fidelity, stereo quality or platform compliance.
- The 12-listener study covered 43 pairs. A separate 20-listener comparison covered only EQ and reverb subsets; it does not establish broad loud-rock mastering quality.
- Long-form behavior needs a separate gate: not every reverb metric improved on complete songs, and the authors report occasional robotic voice, slight intelligibility loss and inconsistent volume across chained segments.
- Album coherence and format-specific mastering are outside the current model scope.

The official code/model metadata was read. The model is reported as 863M parameters under Apache-2.0. No model or dataset files were downloaded and no Space or GPU Job was launched.

1. gate reference similarity, independent quality/delivery metrics and Dmitry's blind preference separately;
2. curate or reject the reference before increasing transfer strength;
3. log and bypass-test every white-box parameter move;
4. treat genre words as directions to explicit hypotheses, not numeric targets;
5. never promote synthetic corruption ranges as engineering presets;
6. require full-song boundary, ambience, stereo and loudness-continuity checks;

## Handoff for mixing agents

Do not optimize “closer to reference” as if it meant “better master.” Curate the reference first, keep a no-transfer baseline, and gate similarity, independent quality/delivery checks and blind preference separately. Treat genre prompts and synthetic degradation ranges as hypotheses only. Segment-based processing must pass a full-song continuity check before acceptance.
