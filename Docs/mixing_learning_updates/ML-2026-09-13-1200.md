## Literature and reproducible implementations

### SRC-ML-20260913-1200-01 — Diff2Mix

- **Primary source:** Yisu Zong, Jinjie Shi, Joshua Reiss, *Diff2Mix: Controllable Music Mixing via Diffusion Models and Differentiable Audio Effects*, arXiv:2608.05442v1, submitted 2026-08-05; accepted at ISMIR 2026. https://arxiv.org/html/2608.05442v1
- **Reading depth:** full HTML: method, differentiable console, datasets, objective evaluation, subjective test, limitations.
- **System:** reference-conditioned diffusion in an audio-effects embedding space; explicit parameters are rendered through `dasp-pytorch`. Each track uses the same fixed chain: `gain -> parametric EQ -> DRC -> stereo panning -> send reverb`.
- **Data/evaluation:** 196 MedleyDB plus 240 MoisesDB multitracks, 90/10 split; the subjective test used only three multitracks, three references and 20 valid experienced participants.
- **Important result:** the proposed models worked for electropop and soul references but all failed for the rock reference. The authors attribute this to one global style embedding not representing track-specific and inter-track rock relationships.
- **Metric caveat:** FxEncoder++ was involved in training and was also used as one evaluation representation; the authors explicitly say this may not be a fair measure.
- **Limitations:** tiny subjective set; fixed topology/order; a global style control; no evidence yet for robust generalization to diverse rock arrangements. A Gaussian mean used by the distribution decoder is explicitly not guaranteed to be perceptually best.
- **Confidence:** high for the reported experiment, medium for transfer to our system.

### SRC-ML-20260913-1200-02 — autonomous multitrack DRC

- **Primary source:** Jacob A. Maddams, Saoirse Finn, Joshua D. Reiss, *An Autonomous Method for Multi-Track Dynamic Range Compression*, DAFx-12, 2012. https://www.dafx.de/paper-archive/2012/papers/dafx12_submission_6.pdf
- **Reading depth:** full 8-page paper.
- **Method:** cross-adaptive monaural per-track compression controlled by loudness and loudness range; six threshold/ratio × light/medium/heavy modes. The paper used 400 ms loudness windows at 7.5 Hz instead of the standard 3 s LRA window for this experiment.
- **Results:** compression usually reduced per-track LRA, but not always and not predictably enough to infer the final LRA change. In the listening test a light touch generally performed best, yet the no-compression mix was consistently among the preferred variants. No condition averaged above “fair”.
- **Test limits:** 41 tracks from seven songs; only 20-second excerpts; excerpts were selected to have all tracks active, a large reducible LRA spread and audible treatment differences; 15 experienced listeners. Four songs were rock/indie, two jazz, one folk.
- **Interpretation:** `3/6/9 LU` ΔLRA targets and the `400 ms` window are experiment-specific settings, not standards or recommended production presets.
- **Confidence:** high for the paper; medium-low for direct adoption in modern dense rock.

### SRC-ML-20260913-1200-03 — dasp-pytorch code inspection

- **Official source:** https://github.com/csteinmetz1/dasp-pytorch (Apache-2.0). Files inspected on `main`: `README.md`, `dasp_pytorch/functional.py`, `dasp_pytorch/modules.py`.
- **Reading depth:** static source inspection; code was not executed and no audio was rendered.
- **Observed implementation detail:** `compressor()` exposes `release_ms`, the module publishes a release range, but the current inspected `functional.py` comments out `normalized_release_time` and `alpha_R` and states that the release time constant is not used in the smoothing filter. Gain smoothing shown in that function uses the attack coefficient.
- **Limited conclusion:** changing `release_ms` appears unable to change this function’s output in the inspected `main` source. This does **not** establish which exact commit Diff2Mix used and does not apply to versions not inspected.
- **Confidence:** high for the static observation; medium until reproduced with a pinned commit and null test.

### VID-ML-20260913-1200-03 — Adam “Nolly” Getgood: master bus and rock drums

- **Video:** *Mixing Masterclass part 1 of 2: Master bus and drums*. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cgfBcFTbir4
- **Transcript:** complete, English auto captions, 0:02–1:12:25.
- **Context:** an alternative/commercial-rock demonstration using a programmed drum library; this is an engineer’s style and a product demonstration, not a scientific experiment.
- **Classification:** EQ; compression; attack/release/ratio/GR; saturation/clipping; transient control; reverb/room; automation; mix bus; kick/snare/toms/overheads.
- **Selected techniques:**
  - 1:47–8:04: top-down mix bus; fast attack (`0.3 ms`), fastest release, `4:1`, detector HPF. In this example he targets roughly `3–4 dB GR` on snare hits and about `1 dB` on kick, using the HPF to choose how much kick triggers the bus.
  - 26:18–27:21: slow-attack/fast-release kick compression can shorten low-frequency bloom; the dry/wet mix restores part of that bloom while retaining enhanced attack.
  - 33:35–34:57: a close-snare expander shortens the close-mic decay while overhead/room/reverb supply body. Demonstrated range is about `-13 dB`, release about `200 ms`, lookahead about `0.5 ms`.
  - 40:31–43:20: parallel bus receives unequal sends, most prominently snare; it is heavily compressed/saturated to reduce transient and fill the gaps between hits as the gain recovers.
  - 43:28–45:18: reverb is intentionally non-realistic, filtered and compressed; its role is extra snare length and stereo width rather than simulating the recorded room.
  - 1:03:22–1:07:57: room bus is filtered/EQ’d, then compressed; a snare-keyed upward expander restores snare prominence in the room after compression.
  - 1:08:03–1:12:13: softer verse receives less close mic, room, parallel send and reverb; a more mono room texture is favored. Room/drum bus rise into the transition and chorus. This is section automation, not one global drum preset.
- **Numeric provenance:** all listed figures are from auto captions and require visual/plugin-session verification before entering a preset. The captions are especially uncertain around `-13 dB`, `200 ms`, `0.5 ms` and the section automation values.

## What is genuinely new for Mixing Learning

1. A recent controllable automatic-mixing system reported a specific failure of **rock reference-style transfer** when one global embedding was used. Rock needs per-role and inter-track constraints, not merely a global target vector.
2. Editable parameters are not enough for interpretability: every exposed control needs a measured parameter-sensitivity test on the exact pinned implementation.
3. `dasp-pytorch`’s current inspected compressor source provides a concrete regression candidate: `release_ms` is exposed but appears inactive. This is not yet an executed bug reproduction.
4. Cross-adaptive LRA compression needs a mandatory `no DRC` baseline. Reducing between-track LRA spread is not a self-validating goal.
5. The three videos converge on a constrained principle: drum compression must be routed and automated by role and section. They disagree on topology—Frightbox excludes cymbals/rooms from the crushed bus; Nolly feeds small amounts of overhead/room and deliberately varies sends. This is a stylistic/arrangement-dependent choice, not a contradiction to resolve globally.

## Knowledge Cards

- **KC-ML-20260913-1200-01 — Global style is insufficient for rock.** Reference conditioning must preserve kick–bass, shell–cymbal, guitar–vocal and room relationships per section. Confidence: medium-high.
- **KC-ML-20260913-1200-02 — Fixed chain order is a model limitation.** Parameter prediction cannot compensate for an effect that should be absent, reordered or replaced. Confidence: high.
- **KC-ML-20260913-1200-03 — Held-out evaluation is required.** A representation used during training can favor related systems; use independent metrics plus listening. Confidence: high.
- **KC-ML-20260913-1200-04 — Parameter sensitivity precedes interpretability.** An exposed value is only a valid control if changing it measurably changes the render as designed. Confidence: high.
- **KC-ML-20260913-1200-05 — `release_ms` is a regression candidate, not a proven runtime bug.** Static inspection of current `dasp-pytorch/main` shows no active release coefficient. Confidence: medium.
- **KC-ML-20260913-1200-06 — No-compression is a first-class baseline.** In the DAFx test it was consistently competitive; automatic DRC must be allowed to abstain. Confidence: medium-high.
- **KC-ML-20260913-1200-07 — LRA response is signal-dependent.** More DRC usually reduced LRA in the study, but not always or predictably enough to use target LRA spread as sole control. Confidence: high.
- **KC-ML-20260913-1200-08 — Drum-bus membership is a creative parameter.** Shell-only and weighted full-kit parallel buses serve different cymbal/room goals. Confidence: medium-high.
- **KC-ML-20260913-1200-09 — Section automation outranks a single drum preset.** Verse/chorus differences can be created by changing room width/level, parallel send and close-mic prominence. Confidence: medium-high.
- **KC-ML-20260913-1200-10 — Attack, release and wet/dry interact.** Preserve transient, low-end bloom and room continuity by testing them together at matched loudness. Confidence: medium-high.

## Candidate rules

### RULE-ML-20260913-1200-01 — reject global-only rock style transfer

- **Source:** Diff2Mix §5.
- **When:** a reference-conditioned system is asked to match rock/metal production style.
- **Change:** add per-section, per-role targets and constraints; expose a manual override.
- **Do not damage:** arrangement hierarchy, kick–bass relationship, guitar mass, drum attack, vocal intent.
- **Verify:** loudness-matched blind A/B against global-only and manual references; inspect stems and full mix.
- **Undo when:** role constraints reduce overall coherence or contradict the section’s artistic intent.
- **Confidence:** medium-high; `auto_apply:false`.

### RULE-ML-20260913-1200-02 — do not force a fixed effect chain

- **Source:** Diff2Mix fixed-console limitation; prior MEGAMI/RelFx/StemFX cards.
- **When:** the predicted chain adds processing without demonstrated need.
- **Change:** allow effect bypass/selection and order candidates before continuous parameter tuning.
- **Do not damage:** latency, phase, transient shape, spectral intent, automation continuity.
- **Verify:** compare fixed chain, selected chain and manual chain at equal loudness.
- **Undo when:** extra search variance hurts repeatability without audible benefit.
- **Confidence:** high; `auto_apply:false`.

### RULE-ML-20260913-1200-03 — require parameter-sensitivity tests

- **Source:** `dasp-pytorch` static inspection.
- **When:** importing or updating differentiable DSP used for training, inference or rendered settings.
- **Change:** sweep every exposed parameter while holding all others fixed; measure output deltas and null residuals.
- **Do not damage:** deterministic rendering, gradients, numerical stability, channel linking.
- **Verify:** nonzero, directionally plausible changes on synthetic and musical test signals; compare against documentation.
- **Undo when:** the parameter is intentionally inert and clearly removed from the public control surface.
- **Confidence:** high; `auto_apply:false`.

### RULE-ML-20260913-1200-04 — keep `no DRC` as a candidate

- **Source:** Maddams et al., DAFx-12.
- **When:** an auto-compressor cannot demonstrate a section-specific benefit over the untreated balance.
- **Change:** include an abstain/no-compression branch in search and evaluation.
- **Do not damage:** groove, transient identity, phrase dynamics, quiet-detail contrast.
- **Verify:** loudness-matched blind A/B with no DRC and at least one light treatment.
- **Undo when:** untreated dynamics cause clearly repeatable balance or audibility failure.
- **Confidence:** medium-high; `auto_apply:false`.

### RULE-ML-20260913-1200-05 — LRA is advisory, not the target

- **Source:** Maddams et al., DAFx-12.
- **When:** cross-adaptive compression is driven by per-track loudness range.
- **Change:** use LRA/ΔLRA only as a feature; constrain decisions by role, section and perception.
- **Do not damage:** deliberate accent contrast, drum punch, sustained guitar density, vocal/instrument phrasing.
- **Verify:** section-wise listening plus envelope/crest diagnostics; compare to no DRC.
- **Undo when:** LRA guidance repeatedly selects worse blind-rated variants.
- **Confidence:** high; `auto_apply:false`.

### RULE-ML-20260913-1200-06 — choose parallel-bus membership by cymbal/room risk

- **Source:** Frightbox 3:44–5:17; Nolly 40:31–43:20 and 1:02:39–1:02:57.
- **When:** parallel compression makes hi-hat/cymbals jump or room continuity collapse.
- **Change:** compare shell-only, shell-heavy weighted sends and current routing; keep a clean overhead/room path.
- **Do not damage:** cymbal smoothness, snare length, stereo image, kit cohesion.
- **Verify:** matched-loudness A/B in dense chorus and sparse verse; solo only for diagnosis.
- **Undo when:** excluding ambience makes the kit detached or overly sample-like.
- **Confidence:** medium-high; `auto_apply:false`.

### RULE-ML-20260913-1200-07 — tune bus ballistics with detector context

- **Source:** Universal Audio 1:47–5:35; Nolly 3:22–6:37.
- **When:** mix/drum bus compression loses attack or pumps from kick low end.
- **Change:** jointly test attack, release, detector HPF/weighting and wet/dry; do not copy isolated numbers.
- **Do not damage:** snare cut, kick weight, cymbal decay, groove, mix stability.
- **Verify:** matched-loudness A/B; inspect gain-reduction timing separately for kick and snare.
- **Undo when:** control improves meters but reduces blind-rated energy or makes the section smaller.
- **Confidence:** medium-high; `auto_apply:false`.

### RULE-ML-20260913-1200-08 — automate drum ambience by section

- **Source:** Nolly 1:08:03–1:12:13; prior Joe Barresi room card.
- **When:** one static room/parallel setting makes the verse oversized or the chorus undersized.
- **Change:** create section candidates for room level/width, parallel send, close-mic level and reverb; protect transitions.
- **Do not damage:** kit perspective, groove, mono compatibility, phrase continuity.
- **Verify:** blind full-song pass plus section-loop A/B; loudness-match the compared sections.
- **Undo when:** automation is noticeable as a technical jump or competes with the song’s own dynamics.
- **Confidence:** medium-high; `auto_apply:false`.
