### SRC-ML-20260930-1500-01 — Efficient Audio Enhancement with a Differentiable Psychoacoustic Loss

- **Stable IDs:** DOI `10.17743/jaes.2022.0277`; `arXiv:2608.02918` (author-accepted manuscript). The journal article and arXiv manuscript are one version family, not independent confirmations.
- **Publication/discovery:** JAES 74(6), pp. 430–444, published `2026-06-08`; arXiv v1 submitted `2026-08-03`; discovered for this index `2026-09-30`.
- **URLs:** https://doi.org/10.17743/jaes.2022.0277 and https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02918
- **Reading depth:** `full_relevant_text`; abstract, introduction, AERO/Mamba/PAQM method, datasets, evaluation protocol, both experiment result/discussion sections and conclusion read. Supplemental per-pair significance tables and every implementation file were not exhaustively audited.
- **Method read:** AEROMamba replaces AERO attention/LSTM components with Mamba state-space blocks. AEROMamba-P adds differentiable PAQM to adversarial, spectral-reconstruction and feature-map losses; the MP3 variant removes the STFT reconstruction term and keeps PAQM plus time-domain adversarial/feature losses.
- **Data/scope read:** stereo 44.1-kHz PianoEval and MUSDB mixtures; the main tasks are reconstructing 44.1 kHz from 11.025 kHz and enhancing audio encoded at 32 kbps then decoded/downsampled. Isolated MUSDB stems were not used.
- **Listening protocol read:** a MUSHRA-like custom test, not standard MUSHRA: no hidden reference, one anchor, unnumbered 0–100 slider, randomized questions, an acoustically isolated lab and Sennheiser HD 265 headphones. Twenty subjects were recruited per experiment; 12 PianoEval and 12 MUSDB tracks were used, with each signal evaluated by ten subjects.
- **Results read:** for bandlimited MUSDB, mean listening scores are 60.03 (AERO), 66.47 (AEROMamba) and 79.26 (AEROMamba-P). The pattern does not generalize to piano: AEROMamba-HQ scores 84.41 versus 78.76 for AEROMamba-P-HQ, and the authors describe PAQM as counterproductive there. For 32-kbps MUSDB restoration, AEROMamba-P-without-STFT scores 75.6 while low-bitrate, AEROMamba and AEROMamba-P cluster near 50; the corresponding piano systems cluster much more closely.
- **Metric independence read:** PAQM is part of the training objective and validation/model selection, so the study uses ViSQOL plus listening as less-coupled endpoints. ViSQOL and listening can still disagree materially in the MP3 MUSDB condition, so neither is a substitute for listening and explicit signal checks.
- **Efficiency read:** on one RTX 3090 setup, AERO uses 17,091 MB and 1.246 s for a 10-s segment versus 3,000 MB and 0.087 s for AEROMamba. These are implementation/hardware measurements, not a project latency guarantee; AEROMamba-P was not timed separately because it shares the backbone.
- **Limitations:** same-author method/evaluation; task is restoration rather than mixing; AudioSR is excluded from subjective tests and had MUSDB exposure; PAQM weight was not extensively tuned; fixed oversampling/cutoff; exact supplemental significance pairs not fully audited; no independent rock-mixing test.

## Knowledge Cards

### KC-ML-20260930-1500-01 — An optimized metric cannot validate itself

When PAQM is a loss and a model-selection signal, PAQM improvement is coupled to optimization. Keep a less-coupled objective metric, explicit signal diagnostics and a loudness-matched blind listening endpoint. This is an evaluation rule, not evidence that ViSQOL is universally perceptual ground truth.

### KC-ML-20260930-1500-02 — Perceptual-loss benefit is content and degradation dependent

The same PAQM term helps MUSDB bandwidth extension, is counterproductive for the selected piano condition and only becomes decisive for MP3 MUSDB when the STFT reconstruction term is removed. Do not transfer a loss topology or weight across material/tasks without controlled validation.

### KC-ML-20260930-1500-03 — Modified listening tests need their own protocol label

The paper's custom test has no hidden reference and one anchor. Preserve its actual design and participant/excerpt counts; do not report its scores as standard MUSHRA or compare them numerically with a standard MUSHRA study without qualification.

### KC-ML-20260930-1500-04 — Silent rejection of invalid training batches is a bias risk

If gradient failures cause batches to be discarded, log every rejection and compare rejected versus retained content. A low average loss or successful checkpoint is insufficient when the effective training distribution is unknown.

### KC-ML-20260930-1500-05 — Restoration evidence is not a mixing prescription

Bandwidth extension and MP3 artifact removal can change timbre and transients, but the study does not establish better balance, masking control, loudness, width or section-role decisions in a rock mix.

### RULE-ML-20260930-1500-01 — Independent endpoint guard for optimized audio metrics

- **Source:** `SRC-ML-20260930-1500-01`.
- **When:** a learned processor is trained or selected using a perceptual metric.
- **Change:** exclude the optimized metric from sole acceptance; add at least one less-coupled metric, explicit peak/spectrum/transient/stereo checks and loudness-matched blind listening.
- **Settings:** no DSP range; protocol derived from PAQM/ViSQOL/listening disagreement in the source.
- **Protect:** attacks, spectral naturalness, stereo/mono behavior, headroom and section intent.
- **Verify/cancel:** pre-register endpoint roles; cancel adoption if only the optimized metric improves or a protected endpoint regresses.
- **Confidence:** `high_for_evaluation_scope`, `low_for_direct_mixing_transfer`; **auto_apply:** `false`.

### RULE-ML-20260930-1500-02 — Content-stratified restoration gate

- **Source:** `SRC-ML-20260930-1500-01`.
- **When:** auditioning bandwidth extension or lossy-codec restoration on project-owned material.
- **Change:** stratify excerpts by transient density and tonal/sustained content; compare results separately rather than averaging across all excerpts.
- **Settings:** source task boundaries are 11.025→44.1 kHz and 32-kbps MP3; they are provenance, not recommended production settings.
- **Protect:** drum attacks, cymbal texture, vocal consonants when present, guitar bite, noise character and headroom.
- **Verify/cancel:** require no systematic failure in any stratum; cancel on added percussion-like energy, ghost lines, clicks or listener rejection.
- **Confidence:** `medium_within_restoration_scope`, `low_for_mixing`; **auto_apply:** `false`.
