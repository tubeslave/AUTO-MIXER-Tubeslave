### What the paper actually does

DiffVox inverse-fits a fixed mono-input/stereo-output chain: six-band parametric EQ, compressor/expander, dry/wet split, panning, ping-pong delay, a six-delay-line FDN reverb and delay-to-reverb send. The model exposes 152 parameters; 130 form the analysed minimum representation.

The evaluation used 70 retained MedleyDB vocal tracks and 365 retained private/Internal tracks at 44.1 kHz. Input and target were separately normalized to -18 LUFS for fitting. The optimization used 12-second segments with a 5-second warm-up/overlap, loss on the final 7 seconds, up to 35 segments and 2,000 Adam steps at learning rate 0.01. Reported fitting time was 20–40 minutes per track on an RTX 3090. These are method details, **not** project presets.

The paper reports lower multi-scale spectral and local-dynamics losses than the no-processing baseline. Ablations suggest the spatial branch helps the paper's architecture-aligned microdynamics objective. Correlations include delay time versus feedback (`-0.58`), delay time versus delay gain (`-0.51`) and compressor threshold versus makeup gain (`-0.55`). Normality tests on the first 75 PCs (about 99% of variance) gave near-zero p-values, arguing against independent Gaussian or uniform priors for this fitted representation.

### Limits

- No listening experiment in this paper; objective inverse-fit loss is not listener preference.
- Single vocal track, not a full mix; masking and role interactions are absent.
- Fixed static topology; distortion, saturation, modulation and heavy automation are outside scope.
- The large private/Internal audio set is unavailable; genre balance and rights cannot be independently audited.
- Fitted parameters are topology-, data-, normalization- and loss-conditioned proxies, not identifiable copies of the original engineer's settings.
- Problematic runs were excluded using empirical thresholds; no external genre/artist/section holdout is reported.
- Paper means (including values around 2 s reverb, 3.5:1 ratio, -22 dB threshold and 2.4 dB makeup) are dataset/model summaries and must not be copied as rock-vocal presets.

## Knowledge Cards

### `KC-ML-20261012-1500-01` — inverse-fit controls are proxies

Effect parameters recovered under a fixed differentiable chain cannot be treated as the original engineer's true settings. They are one solution conditioned by topology, normalization, objective and optimizer.

### `KC-ML-20261012-1500-02` — independent simple priors are insufficient

The analysed vocal-effects representation is strongly non-Gaussian. Independent uniform/Gaussian parameter sampling is therefore a weak default for proposing realistic presets, but the paper does not validate a universal rock-vocal prior.

### `KC-ML-20261012-1500-03` — preserve parameter coupling

Observed threshold/makeup and delay-time/gain/feedback correlations support testing coherent coupled candidate moves instead of arbitrary independent parameter changes. Correlation is not causation and remains dataset/topology conditioned.

### `KC-ML-20261012-1500-04` — objective spatial benefit is not preference

Spatial effects improved the paper's inverse-fit objectives, especially local dynamics, but this does not establish improved intelligibility, depth or preference in a dense rock mix.

### `KC-ML-20261012-1500-05` — topology gate is mandatory

Do not transfer a rule from this source when the vocal depends on distortion, modulation, strong time-varying automation or full-mix sidechain interactions missing from the fitted chain.
