## Literature source read in full

### SRC-ML-20261007-0900-01 — statistical offline mastering dynamics

- title: *A Statistical Approach to Automated Offline Dynamic Processing in the Audio Mastering Process*
- authors: Marcel Hilsamer; Stephan Herzog
- publication: DAFx-2014, pp. 35–40; publication year `2014`; discovered for this base `2026-10-07`
- stable page: https://www.dafx.de/paper-archive/details/0nvFGZkofIGYGxK8KfTZKA
- full text: https://www.dafx.de/paper-archive/2014/dafx14_marcel_hilsamer_a_statistical_approach_to.pdf
- DOI: none found
- reading depth: complete six-page paper, including model, parameter derivation, both simulation scenarios, informal listening statement, limitations and conclusion
- status: `studied_full_text_peer_reviewed_conference`

The paper changes a compressor's ballistics so that static threshold `T` and ratio `R` can be estimated offline to match selected central moments of the side-chain level distribution. The experiments use five songs available in unmastered/reference forms. The authors first recover known parameters from renders produced by their own topology, then match unmastered songs to either a conventional compressed render or a professionally mastered render. Mean matching is close; variance remains less exact because the finite ballistics and limiter are outside the simplified distribution model.

The stronger claim is not supported: matching long-term RMS-distribution moments is not evidence that transients, section contrast, spectral balance, stereo image or rock aesthetics are preserved. The paper's listening validation is informal, with no listener count, blinding protocol or inferential statistics. Professional masters may include limiting, EQ and multiband processing that a single compressor cannot reproduce. Genre-conditioned targets are asserted as a possibility, not validated as universal genre norms.

Study-specific numbers are retained only as provenance: `48 kHz`, `1024`-sample Hann frames, `512`-sample hop, example maximum attack/release of `100/200 ms`, and limiter example `0.5 ms / 50 ms / -0.1 dB`. A worked example produced `T=-16.5 dB`, `R=1.281`. None of these values is promoted as a production preset or safe range.

No source code, public dataset manifest or formal listening protocol was supplied in the paper. A targeted citation search found later citations but no independently reproduced listening result or correction; absence of a found rebuttal is not confirmation. `citation_check:partial`.

## Knowledge Card

### KC-ML-20261007-0900-01 — distribution matching is an initializer, not acceptance

Long-term detector-distribution moments can constrain an offline compressor search, but they collapse time order and cannot by themselves protect attacks, groove, section contrast, spectral balance or stereo presentation. Treat moment matching as a parameter-initialisation hypothesis; require raw diagnostics, section-stratified checks and randomized loudness-matched listening before accepting a mastering change.

Confidence: `medium` for the mathematical capability within the paper's topology; `low` for direct transfer to rock-mastering preference.
