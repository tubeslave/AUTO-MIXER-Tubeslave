## Literature and official-material Source Cards

### SRC-ML-20261010-0900-01 — reference-free singing pitch correction

- **Stable key:** `arxiv:2610.11524v1`; arXiv-issued DOI `10.48550/arXiv.2610.11524` pending registration.
- **Title:** *Reference-Free Singing Pitch Correction via Music-Constrained Sequence Editing*.
- **Authors:** Biao Dong, Jiajun Li, Binzhen Zhu, Mingwei Yi, Tong Liu, Yuanhao Zhang, Jiqing Han, Yongjun He.
- **Status/date:** preprint submitted 2026-10-08, announced 2026-10-09; submitted to ICASSP 2027; discovered 2026-10-10.
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11524
- **Reading depth:** complete five-page PDF including method, data, baselines, objective/subjective evaluation, ablations and limitations.
- **Method:** SOME transcribes note events, RMVPE supplies frame F0, a frozen MusicBERT backbone with singing adapters predicts edit necessity and one of six non-zero semitone offsets (`±1..±3`), and a soft key posterior reranks seven candidates including no change. Timing is held fixed; corrected audio is resynthesised with NSF-HiFi-GAN.
- **Data:** training uses MIDI extracted from 16,000 professional recordings (about 1,060 h) with synthetic errors on 20% of notes; 70% are independent and 30% spans of two to four notes. Evaluation uses a private paired amateur/professional set: 20 validation songs/8,299 notes and 80 held-out test songs/30,682 notes.
- **Results:** on the authors' held-out paired set, accuracy rises from 73.25% input to 83.43%; repair is 46.81% and harm is 3.19%. BERT-APC reaches 79.44%/26.18%/1.11%. The larger repair gain therefore trades against a higher harm rate. The full model beats both correction baselines in paired song-level Wilcoxon tests with Holm-adjusted `p<0.001`.
- **Listening:** 30 vocally trained participants each rated five random 8–12 s excerpts across four methods with piano-rendered professional references. The proposed method reports the highest mean pitch-correctness and overall scores while naturalness remains near input. The short paper does not report participant demographics, listening environment, randomisation/blinding details, confidence intervals or inferential tests for these subjective scores.
- **Bounded conclusion:** selective edit/no-edit modelling and an input-conditioned tonal prior are credible candidates for reducing unnecessary pitch edits. They do not establish an automatic production rule: professional performance is an evaluation reference, not absolute ground truth, and the private data/code/weights prevent independent replication.
- **Relationship:** dependent comparator relationship to existing `SRC-ML-20260915-1200-01` (BERT-APC); not independent confirmation of the MusicBERT/context premise.

## Knowledge Cards

### KC-ML-20261010-0900-01 — pitch correction needs a harm metric

Improved correction/repair can coexist with more damage to already-correct notes. A selection gate must track both repair and harm, not accuracy alone. Confidence: `high_within_reported_protocol`.

### KC-ML-20261010-0900-02 — professional performance is not absolute pitch truth

The paper explicitly treats aligned professional performances as evaluation references rather than absolute ground truth. Expressive alternatives remain valid. Confidence: `high_full_text`.

### KC-ML-20261010-0900-03 — synthetic perturbations do not establish production-error coverage

Training errors affect 20% of notes and are restricted to `±1..±3` semitones with a fixed independent/span mixture. This is a training distribution, not a production correction range. Confidence: `high_full_text`.

### KC-ML-20261010-0900-04 — headroom trim does not reverse limiting

Lowering a pre-limited file prevents further overload but cannot reconstruct clipped/limited transient information. Subsequent clip-gain edits are arrangement-envelope changes and must be judged separately. Confidence: `medium_official_engineer_source_plus_signal_logic`.

### KC-ML-20261010-0900-05 — M/S mastering moves require band/section and mono guards

A side-only high-shelf cut may reduce haze in one dense-metal master, but it can also reduce width, cymbal air and guitar edge. Test it separately from any mid boost, by band and section, with mono and level-matched checks. Confidence: `medium_engineer_demonstration`.

## Candidate Rules

### RULE-ML-20261010-0900-01 — conservative review of reference-free pitch edits

- **Apply when:** a project-owned isolated vocal has no trustworthy score/accompaniment reference and a human editor has approved the intended melodic context.
- **Change:** propose a sparse, frozen note-centre edit map; do not change timing, formants, vibrato depth, dynamics or vocal level in the same comparison.
- **Range provenance:** the paper's `±1..±3` semitone set is model label support, not an adopted correction range; project edits must be chosen from musical evidence.
- **Protect:** intentional bends/blue notes, vibrato, transitions, phrasing, timbre/formants, consonants and section role.
- **Verify:** edit/harm counts, note/F0 diagnostics, raw and BS.1770-matched blind A/B, and Dmitry's intent/naturalness preference.
- **Cancel:** any approved expressive note is normalised away, artifacts/formant shifts appear, or preference disappears after level matching.
- **Confidence:** `medium_method / low_project_transfer / very_low_artifact_readiness`.
- **auto_apply:** `false`.

### RULE-ML-20261010-0900-02 — repair the energy arc before adding master-bus dynamics

- **Apply when:** an already limited final mix has a chorus/build whose musical lift is suppressed, and an un-limited premaster cannot be obtained.
- **Change:** one frozen clip-gain envelope on the transition/section; no compressor, limiter, EQ or stereo change.
- **Range provenance:** no default dB range is adopted; the article's quarter-dB to 1 dB examples are session-specific.
- **Protect:** vocal continuity, transients, ambience tails, edit/crossfade integrity, headroom and approved balance.
- **Verify:** raw and BS.1770-matched blind A/B, section loudness trajectory, true peak, crest/PLD-S, edit clicks and Dmitry's lift/continuity preference.
- **Cancel:** audible edits, verse/introduction leap, transient loss, headroom regression, or preference only at the louder level.
- **Confidence:** `medium_engineer_demonstration`.
- **auto_apply:** `false`.

### RULE-ML-20261010-0900-03 — side-HF reduction as a dense-metal diagnostic

- **Apply when:** a dense section has excessive side-channel cymbal/guitar HF energy and adequate centre punch.
- **Change:** audition one gentle side-only high-shelf cut; do not add the article's mid dynamic boost in the same trial.
- **Range provenance:** no frequency/gain preset is published or adopted; choose one frozen setting in a calibration pass.
- **Protect:** stereo width, cymbal air, guitar edge, snare attack, mono balance and section contrast.
- **Verify:** raw and BS.1770-matched A/B, band-limited M/S balance, mono fold-down, correlation, true peak, crest/PLD-S and Dmitry's clarity/width preference.
- **Cancel:** narrowing, dull cymbals, smaller guitars, weaker chorus contrast, mono imbalance or no matched-level preference.
- **Confidence:** `medium_engineer_demonstration`.
