### Method audit

1. The model is explicitly limited to LTI room IRs. Saturated, fast-compressed, strongly spectrally shaped, spring, and plate responses were removed. This boundary is useful, but the paper does not report the numerical nRMSE exclusion threshold, the rejection count by source library, or source-stratified sampling.
2. All 50 descriptors were calculated from the **resynthesised** IRs, not the original recordings. This keeps targets internally consistent with the modal representation, but weakens any claim that the regressions independently preserve the original IR perceptually or acoustically.
3. PCA, robust scaling, and ten IRLS regressions use the 1,151 retained IRs. Reported `R²rob` is calculated on IRLS inliers from that corpus. No held-out set, cross-validation, source-library split, confidence interval, or external corpus is described; the published scores are therefore in-sample fit diagnostics, not out-of-sample performance.
4. The three demonstration presets are projected into the same six-dimensional descriptor space used to specify and construct them. Their location inside the corpus cloud is a useful range check but not an independent perceptual or generalisation test.
5. Mode count is deliberately remapped to 1,000–8,000 for bounded compute. Mode frequency and phase are stochastic. Any A/B involving diffusion or regenerated IRs must therefore freeze the implementation revision, random seed, mode bank, and phase draw.
6. The amplitude constant `κ≈4.86` has reported standard deviation `≈7.07`, and its gain is absorbed by output normalisation. It must not become a transferable gain setting.
7. The early-reflection shape is a single corpus-mean 256-tap FIR template. `earlyLate` changes its energy relative to the tail through C80; it does not generate geometry-specific early-reflection timing or direction.
8. There is no formal listening test. Real-time performance is an author report for a MATLAB prototype at 44.1 kHz, not reproduced here.

### Revised bounded conclusion

The paper remains a useful synthesis design hypothesis and a source of explicit domain constraints. It does not yet support claims of out-of-sample prediction, room realism, perceptual preference, or production-readiness. Its normalized controls and constants are not mixing presets.

## Knowledge Cards added/revised

1. **In-sample fit is not generalisation.** `R²rob` on IRLS inliers from the fitted corpus cannot be reported as held-out accuracy.
2. **Representation-conditioned descriptors.** Features extracted from the resynthesis measure fidelity within the chosen modal representation, not independent fidelity to the original IR.
3. **Corpus-cloud placement is a range check.** A preset generated from the same six coordinates and then projected back into those coordinates does not independently validate sound quality.
4. **Early/late control has a fixed-shape boundary.** Scaling a mean early FIR changes energy balance but cannot reproduce room-specific early-reflection geometry.
5. **Randomness is an A/B confound.** Diffusion jitter and random modal phases require a frozen seed/mode bank whenever another factor is compared.
6. **Output-normalized constants are non-transferable.** The reported amplitude constant cannot be reused as a production gain value.
