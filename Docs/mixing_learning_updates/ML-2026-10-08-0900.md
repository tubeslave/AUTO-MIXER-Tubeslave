## Source Card

### SRC-ML-20261008-0900-01 — iZotope crest-factor tutorial

- title: *What Is Crest Factor and Why Is It Important?*
- author: Ian Stewart
- publisher/date: iZotope, `2020-07-28`
- URL: https://www.izotope.com/community/blog/what-is-crest-factor
- source type: `official_vendor_educational_material`
- reading depth: `full_web_article_definition_interpretation_mastering_use_low_frequency_section_limitations`
- independence: `vendor-authored; product examples and lookup-table claims are not independent validation`

The article distinguishes the strict steady-state peak/RMS ratio from music-production practice, where engineers may subtract an average measure such as RMS or LUFS from sample peak or true peak over a chosen window. Those choices can yield different values. It repeatedly warns that crest factor is diagnostic rather than a quality score and that comparisons become meaningful only with programme and section context.

The article supplies illustrative ranges for unprocessed signals and genres and describes fast versus longer compressor timing as capable of moving crest factor in different directions. These numbers and directions are retained only as publisher guidance. They are not universal rock-mastering targets, were not linked to a controlled listening study, and must not be used to auto-set compression or limiting.

## Knowledge Card

### KC-ML-20261008-0900-01 — crest factor requires an explicit measurement contract

Store crest factor only with peak type, average-level type, time window, frequency range, channel aggregation and section label. Use it to explain a change between matched versions of the same material, not to rank unrelated songs or declare quality. Confidence: `high` for the definition/metadata requirement; `low` for transferring the vendor's illustrative numeric ranges.
