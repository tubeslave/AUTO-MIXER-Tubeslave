## Literature cards

### `SRC-ML-20260928-0900-01` — Real-Time Music Source Separation on a Low-Power Audio DSP

- **Stable ID:** `arXiv:2609.12201v1`
- **Primary source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12201
- **Version/date:** v1, 10 September 2026; submitted to ICASSP 2027.
- **Reading depth:** complete five-page paper: embedded budget, architecture, continuous-context training, dataset/metrics, on-device timing, ablations and limitations.
- **Method:** causal TFC-TDF U-Net, `1024`-point STFT, `512`-sample hop, GRU state, per-source gated complex FIR deep filter and optional future frames. Training/evaluation use MUSDB18-HQ; deployment is a hand-scheduled float runtime on an Analog Devices SHARC-FX-class target.
- **Measured deployment:** the reported full-band model uses 131k parameters and 21.9M MAC/frame, runs in 10.43 ms mean / 10.44 ms worst case inside an 11.6 ms frame deadline, and occupies 1963/2040 kB L2 and 447/512 kB L1. These are paper measurements on the named target, not transferable project-host guarantees.
- **Streaming failure:** a checkpoint scoring 3.93 dB in reset block-wise evaluation fell to 0.04 dB and near-silent output within about two seconds under continuous frame-by-frame inference. A model trained on continuous 500-frame segments held 3.17 dB over the reported eight-track trajectory; carrying recurrent state alone did not fix the zero-padded convolution-context mismatch.
- **Latency/quality trade-off:** the strictly causal deep filter (`Q=0`) improved the matched full-band model by 0.38 dB cSDR; one future frame added 0.19 dB; two future frames added 23.2 ms to the 23 ms analysis window. On the slim cropped model, most of the gain required the two-frame look-ahead.
- **Metric boundary:** the paper's plain-energy cSDR differs from BSSEval-v4 by -1.33 to +1.21 dB per stem on the same 50 test tracks, with inconsistent sign. Cross-paper SDR values cannot be compared without the exact implementation.
- **Limits:** the deployed model remains 0.47 dB behind RT-STT on the authors' metric and 0.69 dB behind under like-for-like BSSEval-v4. Baseline compute is partly inferred, two slim deep-filter timing rows are projected, fixed-point accuracy and energy are unreported, and domain robustness beyond MUSDB18-HQ is untested. The promised profiler/runtime was not linked on the arXiv record during this run.
- **Limited conclusion:** require continuous-stream evaluation, explicit state/cache semantics, exact metric implementation and measured target-host worst-case timing before treating any separator as deployable. Do not infer mix-control quality or production preference from SDR.

### `EXP-ML-20260928-0900-01` — continuous-context streaming parity

- **Baseline A:** checkpoint trained on independently zero-padded chunks.
- **Variant B:** identical architecture trained on continuous 500-frame segments; training context is the sole factor.
- **Controls:** same train/test split, augmentation, optimiser, seed set, steps, precision, weights count, STFT/hop, cache implementation and checkpoint-selection rule.
- **Run:** uninterrupted frame-by-frame inference for at least two minutes plus the paper's reset block-wise protocol.
- **Measure:** per-second per-stem cSDR/uSDR with exact implementation, output/reference energy ratio, silence ratio, state/cache extrema, additive residual, discontinuities, sample/true peak and xrun/deadline misses on target hardware.
- **Listening:** preserve raw renders for technical checks; use separate BS.1770-matched, randomized copies for preference/artifact scoring.
- **Protect:** continuity, attacks, ambience, stereo/mono, non-target leakage and headroom.
- **Success:** continuous inference remains within a preregistered tolerance of block-wise performance without resets or drift.
- **Cancel:** exact training/evaluation implementation is unavailable, or any factor besides training context differs.

### `EXP-ML-20260928-0900-02` — causal versus one-frame look-ahead deep filter

- **Baseline A:** `Q=0` causal deep filter.
- **Variant B:** `Q=1`; look-ahead is the sole factor.
- **Controls:** identical checkpoint family, backbone, filter order, crop, weights/precision, reconstruction, material and host.
- **Measure:** cSDR/uSDR, per-stem deltas, measured end-to-end/worst-case latency, callback margin, memory, additivity, phase, sample/true peak and blind artifact/preference score.
- **Loudness:** raw renders for signal and safety metrics; separate loudness-matched randomized copies for listening.
- **Protect:** live-monitor latency, attacks, stereo integrity, separation continuity and headroom.
- **Cancel:** the implementation changes architecture or retrains other factors between `Q` states, or latency exceeds the intended use case.

### `EXP-ML-20260928-0900-03` — crushed-room contribution in modern rock drums

- **Baseline A:** fixed room-mic EQ/routing, compressor bypassed.
- **Variant B:** identical chain with one verified heavy-compression state; compressor state is the sole factor.
- **Controls:** same excerpt, kit balance, sample/trigger state, polarity/alignment, master chain and automation; first visually verify attack/release because ASR is contradictory.
- **Measure:** raw room/drum-bus GR, event attack/body, room decay, cymbal crest/splash, stereo correlation, mono residual, sample/true peak and section-conditioned blind preference.
- **Loudness:** operational raw comparison plus separate BS.1770-matched randomized full-mix copies.
- **Protect:** kick/snare impact, cymbal smoothness, room continuity, groove, width and headroom.
- **Success:** added ambience/energy is preferred in the intended section without transient, cymbal, image or peak penalty.
- **Cancel:** settings cannot be verified, the effect wins only by level, or dense sections smear.
