- **0:02–0:46, mix-bus EQ:** a broad “happy face” Pultec-style EQ; Scheps states `+2.5 dB at 100 Hz` and a very broad `+4 dB at 10 kHz`, almost shelf-like. These are settings of this mix, not recommendations.
- **1:35–3:32, limiter:** Oxford limiter input reduced by `0.25 dB`, ceiling `-0.1 dB`, slowest attack, fastest release, auto gain/auto comp off. In this mix it reaches roughly `4–5 dB` gain reduction on kick/snare peaks and changes their apparent length as well as peak level.
- **4:24–5:24, drum layout:** traditional close mics plus selective room and ambient reamp; hat/ride spot mics are unused and room is used only in places.
- **5:38–6:57, drums-dirt parallel path:** high-pass EQ → lo-fi clipping → heavier clipping in iZotope Trash → post-EQ removing created midrange harshness. The path is automated mainly into pushes and choruses.
- **6:34–8:24, section dynamics:** the parallel dirt lets cymbals/hat and kit keep up in dense sections; the dry kit itself stays at the same level while added parallel density thickens underneath. Room tracks also enter for pushes. Scheps presents this as subtle movement, not an always-on preset.

### RULE-ML-20261001-1800-01 — Section-automated drum-density candidate

- **Source:** `VID-ML-20261001-1500-01`.
- **When to apply:** only when a dense build/chorus makes a deliberately dark or dry rock kit lose perceived size, while the dry balance and groove already work.
- **What to change:** audition a fixed parallel drum-dirt bus and automate only its contribution into the target sections; keep the dry drum bus unchanged.
- **Candidate range / origin:** no transferable amount is given. The source topology is HPF → clipping/saturation → post-EQ; drive, cutoff, EQ and send level must be set by the local signal. Spoken mix-bus values are not used as defaults.
- **Protect:** kick/snare transient identity, cymbal smoothness, groove, kit width, room coherence, vocal/guitar balance and true-peak headroom.
- **Verify:** raw safety render plus randomized loudness-matched A/B of identical sections; log bus gain, loudness offset, true peak, crest factor, spectral delta and stereo correlation.
- **Cancel:** preference disappears after level matching; cymbals become brittle; clipping/pumping is audible; kit loses articulation or masks section-defining instruments.
- **Confidence:** `medium_single_engineer_demonstration`.
- **auto_apply:** `false`.

- **Success:** B is consistently preferred for density/excitement after loudness matching, with no protected-quality or peak/headroom regression.
- **Cancel:** B wins only when louder, or introduces harshness, pumping, transient blur, image collapse, masking or boundary clicks.
