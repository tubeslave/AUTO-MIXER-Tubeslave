### SRC-ML-20261017-1500-01 — Plate-reverb parameter estimation from multi-resolution spectra

- Stable ID: `conference:dafx:2026:DAFx26_challenge_80`
- Title: *A Multi-Resolution Spectrogram Approach for Estimating the Physical Parameters of a Plate Reverb*
- Authors: Jared Lipkin, Meiying Chen, Benjamin R. Thompson, Andrea Cogliati, Michael C. Heilemann and David A. Anderson
- Publication: DAFx 2026 Parameter Estimation Challenge, Task A; CC BY 4.0
- URL: https://dafx.de/paper-archive/2026/papers/DAFx26_challenge_80.pdf
- Publication date: 2026-09-01/04; discovery date: 2026-10-17
- Reading depth: `full_text_complete_3_pages`
- Method: ResNet-18 receives three magnitude-spectrogram resolutions plus one phase channel. The submitted model downsamples the IR to emphasize isolated low-modal-overlap resonances. The paper compares full-band, downsampled and downsampled-plus-phase variants.
- Reported result: on the paper's controlled synthetic challenge data, downsampling improved mean normalized parameter error relative to the full-band model, and adding phase improved output-location estimation without a material inference-time penalty in the reported small tests.
- Bounded conclusion: relative phase can help resolve magnitude-symmetry ambiguities in controlled plate-IR parameter estimation, and selecting the physically informative low-modal-overlap band can matter more than retaining the full bandwidth.
- Limitations: synthetic challenge distribution; small reported validation subsets in tables; no real plate recording validation; no listener test; no mix-quality outcome; hardware/runtime figures are condition-specific.
- Citation check: `partial`.

### SRC-ML-20261017-1500-02 — Diffusion warm initialization for audio-to-audio transformation

- Stable ID: `conference:dafx:2026:DAFx26_paper_50`
- Title: *Audio-to-Audio via Diffusion Warm Initialization*
- Authors: Cristóbal Andrade and Sebastian J. Schlecht
- Publication: DAFx 2026; CC BY 4.0
- URL: https://dafx.de/paper-archive/2026/papers/DAFx26_paper_50.pdf
- Publication date: 2026-09-01/04; discovery date: 2026-10-17
- Reading depth: `full_text_targeted_sections` — abstract, framework, metrics, applications, discussion and conclusion read; references and all intermediate result lines not exhaustively read.
- Method: reverse diffusion starts from a guide signal at an intermediate time. The paper evaluates a realism/faithfulness trade-off using LAION-CLAP FAD and pitch-set Jaccard distance, then illustrates timbre transfer, MIDI-to-real synthesis and enhancement.
- Bounded conclusion: initialization time, guidance and optional noise injection are coupled model-specific controls. The reported operating point near `tau_init≈0.8` is explicitly limited to Stable Audio Open and must not be copied as a generic audio-processing value.
- Important negative result: the method often failed to suppress unwanted sources, especially percussive/drum components, and could turn them into artificial or distorted remnants. Human-voice guides also produced weaker results.
- Limitations: no mixing-specific evaluation; no controlled listener study; FAD and pitch-set distance do not protect transients, stereo image, groove, source identity or arrangement; model- and task-specific hyperparameters; theoretical selection of initialization time remains open.
- Citation check: `partial`.
