### SRC-ML-20261007-1500-01 — accessibility-oriented level and spectrum transforms

- title: *Exploring level- and spectrum-based music mixing transforms for hearing-impaired listeners*
- authors: Aravindan Joseph Benjamin; Kai Siedenburg
- publication: *Journal of the Acoustical Society of America* 154(2), 1048–1061; `2023`
- DOI: `10.1121/10.0020269`; PMID: `37607002`
- official metadata/abstract: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37607002/
- open full-text URL discovered but not read this run: https://pubs.aip.org/asa/jasa/article-pdf/154/2/1048/18092580/1048_1_10.0020269.pdf
- reading depth: `metadata_and_abstract_only`
- status: `queued_full_text`

The abstract reports two experiments comparing normal-hearing and hearing-impaired listeners on lead-to-accompaniment level ratio, low/high spectral balance and an EQ transform. Hearing-impaired participants preferred a higher lead-to-accompaniment ratio; abstract-level results did not support a distinct general spectral-balance or EQ-transform preference. Hearing-aid use/disuse changed preferences in the second experiment.

Bounded conclusion: the paper is relevant to accessibility-targeted alternate mixes, not a universal instruction to raise vocals or leads in rock. Sample sizes, stimuli, exact adjustment ranges, statistics and limitations have not yet been checked from the full text. No numeric rule was accepted. `citation_check:partial`.

### KC-ML-20261007-1500-02 — rock guitar width can be arranged, not only widened

The Mike Pepe article uses distinct parts and section-dependent positions to occupy the stereo field instead of duplicating every guitar hard L/R. This is an engineering hypothesis: the useful variable is role allocation across centre/intermediate/edge positions, while mono compatibility and centre masking remain constraints.

Confidence: `medium` for faithful extraction from the companion article; `low_to_medium` for transfer to another arrangement.

### KC-ML-20261007-1500-03 — opposite-side ambience is a bounded balance option

For an asymmetrical lead, a mono ambience return on the opposite side can balance width while preserving the direct source location. It is not a universal rule and must be rejected when it destabilises mono, distracts from the hook or creates section-to-section image jumps.

Confidence: `medium` for source extraction; `low_to_medium` for transfer.

## Candidate rules

### RULE-ML-20261007-1500-01 — role-based placement for layered rock guitars

- source: `VIDEO-ML-20261007-1500-01` companion article
- apply when: a chorus has several functionally distinct guitar layers and hard-L/R duplication leaves a centre/intermediate image hole or poor part separation
- what to change: assign clear roles before processing; test wide rhythms, a section-limited centre texture and intermediate octave/lead positions
- proposed range: article-specific starting positions only — rhythms hard L/R, octaves about 75% L/R, leads about 30/60%, centre texture at C; numbers are reported from this production, not scientific limits
- instrument/section scope: layered rhythm/lead guitars, only in sections whose arrangement needs the density; never automatically prioritise vocal or lead guitar
- protect: mono compatibility, kick/snare/bass and chosen lead readability, left/right energy balance, hook clarity, section lift and true-peak headroom
- verify: compare mono fold-down, M/S energy by band, stereo correlation, centre masking and Dmitry's loudness-matched section ratings
- success: guitar roles remain identifiable and the large section feels wider/denser without centre congestion or mono loss
- cancel: centre becomes crowded, edge layers disappear in mono, width is unstable, a support line steals focus or improvement is loudness-only
- confidence: `low_to_medium`
- status: `candidate`
- auto_apply: `false`

### RULE-ML-20261007-1500-02 — opposite-side mono ambience for a panned lead

- source: `VIDEO-ML-20261007-1500-02` companion article
- apply when: a hard-panned lead feels spatially unbalanced but moving or widening the dry source would weaken the arrangement
- what to change: pan only a mono reverb return opposite the dry lead; keep dry pan, send, reverb type/decay/pre-delay/filters and all other processing fixed
- proposed range: test centred return versus fully opposite return; no transferable send-level or decay number was established by the source
- instrument/section scope: exposed lead guitar or similarly asymmetrical melodic support, checked in every section where the send is active
- protect: dry-source localization, mono compatibility, centre clarity, width continuity, lyric/hook focus and headroom
- verify: raw and BS.1770-loudness-matched randomized copies, mono fold-down, stereo correlation, side-energy balance and Dmitry's three-repeat ratings
- success: the image feels balanced while the direct lead remains localized and no protected quality regresses
- cancel: mono loss, phasey image, centre smear, distraction from the intended lead, section jump or preference explained only by loudness
- confidence: `low_to_medium`
- status: `candidate`
- auto_apply: `false`
