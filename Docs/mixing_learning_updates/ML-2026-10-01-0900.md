### SRC-ML-20261001-0900-01 — Using the Distribution Derivative Method to Model Acoustic Musical Instrument Sounds with Polynomial AM-FM Sinusoids

- **Stable ID:** `conference:dafx:2026:DAFx26_paper_34`; archive record `UNNytCTePoNgsd28yb9K7A`
- **Author/publication:** Marcelo Caetano; DAFx-2026, Cambridge, 1–4 September 2026
- **URLs:** https://dafx.de/paper-archive/details/UNNytCTePoNgsd28yb9K7A and https://dafx.de/paper-archive/2026/papers/DAFx26_paper_34.pdf
- **Licence:** paper states CC BY 4.0.
- **Discovery date:** `2026-10-01`
- **Reading depth:** `full_relevant_sections`; abstract, implementation overview, evaluation design, results, post-hoc analysis and conclusion read. Equations were inspected for method context, but the work was not reimplemented and companion audio was not independently scored.
- **Method actually read:** DDM estimates cubic polynomial AM-FM sinusoids inside analysis frames and was compared with SM+ and eaQHM on 39 forte/fortissimo monophonic instrument sounds across sustained, percussive and modulated groups. Objective evaluation used RMS log-spectral measure, Itakura–Saito divergence and signal-to-residual ratio; subjective evaluation used 18 selected sounds in a within-subject MUSHRA design.
- **Results actually read:** DDM generally produced higher signal-to-residual ratio, while eaQHM could look better on some spectral-distance results. The paper reports a significant model effect and Holm–Bonferroni-corrected pairwise tests, but 13 participants entered and 8 were rejected by hidden-reference/anchor screening, leaving only 5 listeners in the analysis. DDM remained significantly below the reference (`p=0.0091`) while exceeding eaQHM (`p=2.7e-9`) in the reported right-tailed comparisons.
- **Bounded conclusion:** metric rankings can diverge when one model captures oscillatory/nonstationary energy while another optimizes spectral fit. For Mixing Learning this supports a multi-axis transient-preservation protocol, not DDM deployment and not a compressor preset.
- **Limits:** monophonic isolated acoustic sounds, strong dynamics only, fixed peak selection and cubic model, no rock mix, no polyphonic full mix, no compression/limiting intervention, very small retained listener sample, no independent replication found in this search.

## Knowledge Cards

### KC-ML-20261001-0900-01 — Spectral distance and transient preservation are separate axes

In the reviewed DAFx experiment, spectral-distance and signal-to-residual rankings were not interchangeable. A mixing or mastering A/B that may alter attacks should therefore report an onset/time-domain endpoint as well as spectral and loudness endpoints.

### KC-ML-20261001-0900-02 — Listener screening changes the effective evidence size

The paper's MUSHRA result used 5 retained listeners after rejecting 8 of 13. The corrected statistics are useful within that experiment, but transfer confidence must remain limited; the abstract's 18 refers to selected sounds, not listeners.

### KC-ML-20261001-0900-03 — Isolated-sound modeling does not establish mix-bus behavior

Better resynthesis of monophonic acoustic instruments does not demonstrate better compression, limiting, crest management or rock-mix preference. It can inform protected measurements only.

### KC-ML-20261001-0900-04 — Metadata-only video cards carry no technique evidence

Titles and descriptions justify queue priority, not settings. All three new cards remain unstudied until a permitted transcript or direct content review is available.

## Limited rule candidate

### RULE-ML-20261001-0900-01 — Multi-axis transient-preservation gate

- **Source:** `SRC-ML-20261001-0900-01`
- **When to apply:** a proposed compressor, limiter, clipper, transient shaper or learned processor may change attacks or crest behavior.
- **What to change:** add an onset/time-domain endpoint and loudness-matched blind listening beside spectral, loudness and peak measurements; never accept from one metric alone.
- **Setting range:** none; evaluation-protocol candidate only. No DDM parameters or study thresholds transfer to the processor.
- **Protect:** drum attack, cymbal texture, guitar pick definition, bass articulation, lead intelligibility, stereo/mono behavior and true-peak headroom.
- **Verify:** raw render safety checks plus randomized BS.1770-matched A/B, with section-stratified onset/crest and listener ratings.
- **Cancel:** metric preference reverses across endpoints, level matching changes the preference, or any protected quality regresses materially.
- **Confidence:** `high_for_evaluation_structure`; `low_for_mixing_transfer`.
- **auto_apply:** `false`
