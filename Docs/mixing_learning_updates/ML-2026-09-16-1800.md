## Source upgraded to full review

`S-ML-20260916-1500-02` — Anthony Gallien, Benoit Alary and Markus Noisternig, *Parametric Resynthesis of Measured Spatial Room Impulse Responses*, DAFx-26, September 2026. [Official record](https://dafx.de/paper-archive/details/nXNFxdUAPViBJLCUKWHxAQ) · [CC BY 4.0 paper](https://dafx.de/paper-archive/2026/papers/DAFx26_paper_08.pdf) · [official companion examples](https://dafx26-eac.ircam.fr/)

### What the method actually does

1. SMA signals are encoded to Higher-Order Ambisonics, acquisition pre-delay is removed, and an estimated mixing time separates spatially coherent direct/early response from stochastic late reverberation.
2. Early reflections are analyzed in short overlapping windows with Herglotz analysis, then represented by count, direction of arrival, time of arrival and extracted spectrum. The synthesis additionally models residual low-frequency rigid-sphere scattering and high-frequency spatial aliasing of the measurement array.
3. Late reverberation estimates frequency-dependent `RT60(f)` and initial decay power `P0(f)`. FDN/DFDN attenuation and output filters reproduce those targets; a third-octave covariance transform imposes the measured late-field spatial relation.
4. Directional decay profiles are clustered so fewer DFDNs can cover the field, while initial decay power remains direction-dependent.

Source-specific values are preserved only as method facts: 48 kHz, 128-sample early windows (reported as 2.6 ms), a 20-sample look-back before estimated ToA, fourth-order EM32 geometry with approximately 5.2 kHz aliasing onset, and a reported late example using five 32-delay-line DFDNs with random delays from 30–70 ms. These are **not** rock-room presets.

### Evaluation and evidence limits

The paper compares reconstruction to measurement using spherical correlation in 5.3-ms blocks and binaurally encoded EDR-difference maps. The detailed paper result is one EM32 measurement in the IRCAM Espro hall. It shows better objective agreement than direct early-plane-wave resynthesis plus uncorrelated FDN output.

Important limits:

- There is no controlled listener study; the conclusion explicitly leaves perceptual thresholds and auralization validation for future work.
- Results are figures rather than a tabulated multi-room statistical evaluation with effect sizes.
- The late model assumes one exponential decay slope per directional band; coupled-room and other multi-slope decays are outside the method.
- Finite SMA order can miss reflections. Adjacent analysis beams may overlap and overestimate power. Resynthesis cannot recover spatial resolution absent from the measurement.
- No platform, CPU load, real-time factor or end-to-end latency table is given, and no source-code repository is linked.

The official companion page contains binaural dry/measurement/direct-resynthesis/proposed-method demos across seven room/configuration sections and three dry source types. This is a demonstration set, not a formal listener test. Its text documents two useful failure cases: the authors describe a large EDR error from multi-slope decay in the Chaillot example as not highly perceptible in their informal listening, while missed salient early reflections in the Athénée example are described as audible in the drum demo. The audio itself was not downloaded and no project metrics were calculated.

## Revised project conclusions

1. **Mixing time is not creative pre-delay.** In this method it is an estimated boundary between coherent early structure and the stochastic late field, after acquisition pre-delay is removed. Moving an entire room return is not equivalent.
2. **RT60 is not a complete room match.** Frequency-dependent decay, initial late-field energy and channel/spatial covariance are separate descriptors.
3. **Objective fit and audibility can disagree in both directions.** A larger decay-map error need not be the most audible defect; omitted salient early reflections can matter more on drums.
4. **SMA artifact modeling is domain-specific.** Rigid-sphere scattering and aliasing belong to reproduction of a known array measurement. They are not generic coloration targets for conventional stereo drum room.
5. **The stereo-rock transfer is a hypothesis.** Early/late separation and evaluation discipline are relevant, but HOA orders, array geometry and reported synthesis values must not enter project presets.

This tightens the earlier `ML-2026-09-13-0900` rule: evaluating early-reflection shape separately from late decay remains supported, but even `RT60 + EDR + covariance` is not sufficient evidence of a preferred musical room without a loudness-matched listening test.
