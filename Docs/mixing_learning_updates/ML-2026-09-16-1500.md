## Literature search

### Fully read — McCormack & Välimäki, FFT-Based Dynamic Range Compression

The complete eight-page peer-reviewed SMC 2017 paper was read: conventional feed-forward DRC baseline, proposed time-frequency architecture, implementation, examples, limitations and conclusion. Source card: `S-ML-20260916-1500-01`. [Open paper](https://leomccormack.github.io/sparta-site/docs/help/related-publications/mccormack2017fft.pdf) · [Aalto metadata and CC BY record](https://research.aalto.fi/en/publications/fft-based-dynamic-range-compression/)

What is supported:

- The controller computes gain per time-frequency cell, so threshold, ratio, knee and attack/release may vary by frequency. A spectral-floor parameter is explicitly recommended to prevent harsh attenuation of individual bands.
- The demonstrated real-time implementation used an alias-reduced STFT filterbank, 1024-point FFT, 128-sample hop, extra low-band subdivision, 133 bands, 48 kHz and a fixed 512-sample frame. These are implementation facts, not recommended rock-mixing presets.
- The paper demonstrates high-resolution multiband compression and frequency-dependent sidechain ducking, including a drum-loop sidechain. It also warns about parameter count, latency and computation.
- Example settings were intentionally aggressive so changes would be visible in spectrograms. The paper explicitly recommends more conservative gain reduction for listening.

What is not supported:

- There is no formal listener study establishing preference, transparency or rock-specific improvement.
- Spectrogram examples do not establish reduced masking, better punch or a preferred mix.
- The example thresholds, ratios and attack/release values cannot be transferred as project defaults.
- The companion implementation was not executed in this run; reproducibility remains partial.

### Queued — DAFx-26 spatial room-impulse resynthesis

`S-ML-20260916-1500-02` — Anthony Gallien, Benoit Alary and Markus Noisternig, *Parametric Resynthesis of Measured Spatial Room Impulse Responses*. [DAFx record](https://dafx.de/paper-archive/details/nXNFxdUAPViBJLCUKWHxAQ) · [PDF](https://dafx.de/paper-archive/2026/papers/DAFx26_paper_08.pdf)

Only the official abstract and programme entry were read. The framework separates early reflections, reconstructed through Herglotz analysis, from late reverberation, synthesized by a directional feedback-delay network optimized for attenuation and correlation matching. It reports matches in spatial correlation and Energy Decay Relief, but the full methods, evaluation protocol, perceptual evidence, dataset rights and failure cases were not available through the text extractor in this search run. No rule was created from this source; it is priority 1 for the 18:00 deep review.

## Cards and genuinely new material

Added **2 Source Cards** (one fully read, one abstract-only queued), **5 Knowledge Cards**, **4 bounded rule candidates**, **4 Video Source Cards**, **1 Reference Card** and **4 experiment plans**. Four literature duplicates were removed. No video was counted as studied.

New bounded conclusions:

1. Time-frequency DRC must expose FFT/window/hop, latency, reconstruction and spectral-floor behavior; “frequency-selective” is not a complete specification.
2. Per-band gain limits are anti-artifact controls as well as creative controls. Their useful values must be derived by audio A/B, not copied from illustrative figures.
3. Desired gain-map fit, artifact burden and musical preference are independent outcomes.
4. A free educational multitrack is not automatically licensed for AI training or automated dataset use.

## Next controlled experiment

`EXP-ML-20260916-1500-01` — on one contributor-cleared dense-rock excerpt, compare unchanged, full-band sidechain ducking, a fixed crossover multiband branch and FFT-bin/time-frequency ducking. The only intended factor is frequency selectivity/topology; use the same sidechain and matched detector intent, render separate loudness-matched listening copies, and do not peak-normalize.

Record latency, impulse response, peak change, gain trace by band, temporal smearing and boundary artifacts. Dmitry judges kick attack, bass weight, rhythm-guitar foundation, snare body, cymbal/room continuity, mono stability and full-mix preference blind. Cancel a branch if it cannot preserve the intended gain action, adds audible spectral holes or time smear, destabilizes the drum image, or fails to beat unchanged. Status: `not_run`.

Follow-ups isolate the per-band attenuation floor, FFT/hop resolution and parallel-return blend as separate one-factor tests. No settings from the queued videos are used until their content becomes available.
