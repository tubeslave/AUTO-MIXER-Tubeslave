## Literature and artifact cards

### `SRC-ML-20260927-1500-01` — Gradient Descent Optimization of Room Impulse Responses with Parameter-Efficient Differentiable Feedback Delay Networks

- **Stable ID:** `DAFx-26:paper_10`
- **Primary source:** https://dafx.de/paper-archive/2026/papers/DAFx26_paper_10.pdf
- **Status:** peer-reviewed DAFx 2026; full PDF read, including method, loss, dataset, baselines, ablations, compute discussion and limitations.
- **Method:** a mono 16-delay-line FDN at 48 kHz is fitted to a measured RIR; delays, orthogonal mixing matrix, early-reflection taps, shared PEQ attenuation and post-processing filters are jointly optimised.
- **Evidence boundary:** nine manually selected, low-noise, non-truncated OpenAIR RIRs, onset-aligned and peak-normalised. There is no formal listening test; the perceptual statement is informal.
- **Attribution correction:** the noise-shaped baseline improves strongly when retrained with the proposed nine-term composite loss. The headline difference therefore combines architecture and loss and must not be attributed to architecture alone.
- **Perceptual correction:** trained delays may become near-commensurate and create metallic ringing. The authors recommend fixed delays/fixed Hadamard structure as more robust at a small objective cost.
- **Parameter-transfer limit:** PEQ-10 is a paper-specific compromise for RIR decay matching; it is not a ten-band mix-EQ preset. PEQ-16 did not provide a consistent benefit in this experiment.
- **Deployment limit:** multiplications per sample and state memory do not establish worst-case callback latency. Training ran on an RTX 4070 Super; renderer latency on project target hosts was not tested.
- **Limited conclusion:** the work supports an opt-in RIR-matching experiment, but acceptance must include independent listening, metallic-colouration checks, raw peak/headroom checks and out-of-set rooms.

### `SRC-ML-20260927-1500-02` — Perceptual Optimisation of Loudspeaker-Based Reproduction

- **Stable ID:** `DAFx-26:paper_13`
- **Primary source:** https://dafx.de/paper-archive/2026/papers/DAFx26_paper_13.pdf
- **Accepted manuscript:** https://openresearch.surrey.ac.uk/esploro/outputs/conferencePaper/Perceptual-Optimisation-of-Loudspeaker-Based-Reproduction/99995812002346
- **Status:** peer-reviewed DAFx 2026; full PDF read, including four validation tasks, optimisation/evaluation losses and limitations.
- **Method:** POLAR optimises loudspeaker FIR filters with differentiable localisation, uncertainty, coloration and binaural/IPD losses. “Differentiable” means automatic differentiation through the signal/perceptual models, not a neural-network requirement.
- **Simulation boundary:** free field, ideal omnidirectional point sources, one virtual source, symmetric KU100 HRIRs and integer-sample propagation delays (maximum stated quantisation error 11 microseconds). Multi-source behaviour is assumed through superposition rather than tested with a music scene.
- **Validation correction:** the first tasks recover known amplitude-panning/VBAP, crosstalk-cancellation and beamforming solutions under matched simulation. This is useful implementation validation, not evidence of superior real-room or creative stereo mixing.
- **Circularity correction:** the multi-attribute solution is optimised and evaluated with the same localisation, uncertainty and coloration models. Three non-optimised positions do not remove that model circularity. Loss weights were chosen by trial and error.
- **Missing evidence:** no formal listening test, measured-room robustness, head/listener variability, multi-source interference, true-peak/headroom or FIR-length deployment test.
- **Limited conclusion:** use POLAR as a research framework for constrained spatial optimisation only; do not transfer its result to rock pan/width rules without independent listeners and measured-room/multi-source tests.

### `EXP-ML-20260927-1500-01` — trained-delay metallicity A/B

- **Baseline A:** fixed delays and fixed Hadamard mixing.
- **Variant B:** trainable delays; delay state is the sole factor.
- **Controls:** identical RIR, loss, filters, early-reflection branch, seed set, epochs, gain and renderer revision.
- **Measure:** paper RIR metrics, delay ratios, modal/spectral peaks, EDR/late-field smoothness, sample and true peak, seed spread and blind preference.
- **Loudness:** preserve raw renders for metrics; create separate BS.1770-matched randomized copies for listening.
- **Protect:** decay, non-metallic timbre, attacks, headroom and stereo/mono compatibility of test programme.
- **Success:** the trainable variant improves preregistered metrics without a metallicity or preference penalty across rooms/seeds.
- **Cancel:** near-commensurate modes, ringing, peak regression or preference loss appears.

### `EXP-ML-20260927-1500-02` — architecture-versus-loss attribution

- **Baseline A:** NoiseShaper with its original multi-resolution STFT loss.
- **Variant B:** the identical NoiseShaper with the composite loss; loss is the sole factor.
- **Controls:** same RIRs, implementation, seed set, optimiser budget, gain and rendering.
- **Measure:** objective RIR metrics, seed variance, spectral/decay residual and blind preference.
- **Loudness:** raw for metrics; separate BS.1770-matched copies for listening.
- **Protect:** causal attribution, decay, coloration and peaks.
- **Cancel:** implementation or training budget changes with the loss.

### `EXP-ML-20260927-1500-03` — POLAR independent-endpoint check

- **Baseline A:** evaluate fixed optimised filters with the same perceptual models used in the loss.
- **Variant B:** evaluate the identical filters with an independent listener/model endpoint; evaluator is the sole factor.
- **Controls:** same audio, geometry, filters, level and source positions.
- **Measure:** localisation error, uncertainty, coloration, IPD, preference and rank disagreement.
- **Loudness:** identical raw renders; matched copies only if required for blind preference.
- **Protect:** evaluation independence and timbre.
- **Cancel:** no independent endpoint is available or model identity cannot be separated.

### `EXP-ML-20260927-1500-04` — free-field-to-measured-room robustness

- **Baseline A:** free-field propagation.
- **Variant B:** measured-room transfer functions; propagation model is the sole factor.
- **Controls:** identical source target, filters, listener/head orientation, loudspeaker positions and gain.
- **Measure:** localisation, uncertainty, coloration, IPD, sample/true peak, filter headroom and blind preference.
- **Loudness:** raw for safety; separate BS.1770-matched randomized copies for preference.
- **Protect:** source position, tonal balance, intelligibility, transients and headroom.
- **Cancel:** optimisation loses spatial target, clips, or worsens coloration/preference beyond preregistered bounds.
