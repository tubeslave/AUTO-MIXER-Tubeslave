## Outcome

The complete five-page PADP preprint was re-read at equation, experiment, result and conclusion level. Its useful claim survives only in bounded form: transformations judged near-transparent on a small mono test can expose instability in objective metrics. Several stronger statements are not directly supported: DIF2 was not tested; the APX random assignment/seed is absent; codec, alignment and loudness-compensation implementations are unspecified; the reported metric correlations have no uncertainty analysis; and the same 12 items are used to define perturbation operating ranges and assess metric robustness.

Targeted verification read the official ITU-R BS.1534-3 experimental-design, listener, analysis and reporting sections, plus the official ODAQ paper/repository and benchmark guidance. ITU warns that MUSHRA is intended for intermediate impairments, that mostly 80–100 scores may indicate an invalid test choice, and calls for randomization, replication/screening detail, inferential analysis, effect sizes and 95% intervals. PADP reports means and pooled SRCC but not those uncertainty and interaction details. ODAQ itself is stereo and much broader than PADP's 12 mono-left-channel subset; the exact selected-item list is not disclosed.

No audio was downloaded, played, analyzed or uploaded. No code, metric, model, plug-in, notebook, Job, GPU or external Space was run. No DSP, runtime rule, repository implementation, model or audio asset was changed.

- Directly tested DIF values: `d={1,3,5}`. The text's “potentially DIF2” / “DIF1–2” is interpolation, not direct listener evidence.
- APX: 12 combinations were sampled from 4096 possibilities, but the mapping of combinations to the 12 items and the random seed are not reported. Because APX appears as one listening condition, combination and content effects cannot be separated from the published description.
- SCD: MP3 at 80 kbps with `lambda={0.2,0.6,0.9,1.2}`; encoder/decoder implementation, version and mode are not reported. Therefore the numerical range cannot be transferred across codecs/builds.
- Protocol: 12 ODAQ items converted to mono by taking the left channel; loudness “compensated” after conversion, but method/target are absent. Metric inputs are time- and level-aligned, but the algorithm and tolerance are absent.
- Listeners: post-screening retained `n=9` for SCD and `n=8` for APX/DEL/DIF. No demographics, training description, reproduction level/calibration, presentation randomization, replications, per-item table, 95% intervals or inferential model are reported.
- Transparency: the paper adopts mean difference grade `<10`; it is not an equivalence test or universal inaudibility boundary. MUSHRA is not optimized for near-ceiling/small-impairment claims.
- Internal inconsistency: discussion permits SCD `lambda>=0.9` for less stringent use, while the conclusion says SCD `lambda<=0.9` when critical signals are excluded. Only the four tested values are evidence; do not extrapolate either inequality.
- Metric analysis: pooled SRCC against mean subjective ratings, with no confidence intervals, significance test, held-out perturbation/material split or simple stability baseline. The table is exploratory and not a universal ranking.
- Independence: DeePAQ and PADP share authors/laboratory lineage. DeePAQ's strongest absolute SRCC is not independent confirmation.
- Foundation-model entries use Euclidean embedding distance as an ad hoc probe; they do not evaluate a released audio-quality head and do not rank the base models as production quality metrics.
- “Potential data augmentation” is a future hypothesis; no training/fine-tuning experiment is reported.
- Artifact status: no official PADP code, stimuli list, APX assignment/seed or per-item result matrix found. Exact reproduction remains blocked.
- Revised bounded conclusion: PADP motivates a human-gated robustness check for candidate metrics. It does not establish a mix processor, transparent stereo transform, production default or metric leaderboard.
