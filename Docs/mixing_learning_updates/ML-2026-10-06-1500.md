### Method and data

DeePAQ uses the 95M-parameter MERT-v1 foundation model. Outputs from 13 layers are averaged across time and concatenated to 9,984 dimensions, then projected to 256 dimensions. The model is fine-tuned with LoRA on query/value layers (rank 8, scale 16) and a Rank-n-Contrast objective. Training targets are weak labels derived from ViSQOL MOS plus codec bitrates, not human labels.

The internal training corpus is about 460 hours of 44.1 kHz CD-quality audio, segmented into four-second excerpts and resampled to 24 kHz. AAC, Opus and MP3 distortions cover 16–128 kb/s; the paper reports 122 hours per codec plus 45 hours of clean material and a 50-hour validation set. Evaluation covers nine listening tests spanning audio coding and source separation. The internal corpus, split manifests and public reproduction package were not found.

### Results and bounded interpretation

Selected reported correlations:

| Condition | DeePAQ PCC | DeePAQ SRCC | 2f-model PCC | 2f-model SRCC |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| All test samples, full reference | 0.918 | 0.889 | not used as sole comparator here | not used as sole comparator here |
| Source separation overall, full reference | 0.919 | 0.787 | 0.953 | 0.881 |
| PEASS subset, full reference | 0.859 | 0.467 | 0.898 | 0.624 |
| Source separation overall, non-matching reference | 0.310 | 0.314 | — | — |

The reported aggregate full-reference result is strong, but the simpler two-feature model is stronger on the source-separation aggregate and PEASS subset. The non-matching-reference form collapses on source separation, so it must not be treated as an arbitrary mix-quality or preference gate. This is evidence for domain-specific signal-quality prediction, not for rock-mix aesthetics, arrangement decisions, musical balance or automatic acceptance.

LoRA updates 2.93% of parameters and performs best among the tested fine-tuning strategies under limited data; the gap narrows with more training data. Adding bitrate to the Rank-n-Contrast surrogate gives only about a one-to-three-percent test improvement. Cubic/MLP mappings improve PCC more than SRCC, which supports calibration of the output scale more than improved ordering.

### Limitations

- training distortions are codecs and pseudo-labels inherit ViSQOL/bitrate assumptions;
- source separation is out-of-distribution evaluation relative to codec training;
- full-reference use requires a clean matched reference that arbitrary mixes do not have;
- four-second, 24 kHz excerpts do not test full-song arrangement, long dynamics, stereo space or section intent;
- no rock-mix preference, headroom, transient hierarchy, tonal-balance or creative-intent study;
- the internal 460-hour corpus is not public;
- no public DeePAQ code, licensed checkpoint, split manifest or reproduction output was found;
- the same paper's variants are not independent confirmations.

Confidence: high for reported table values and scope boundaries; low for direct production-mix transfer.
