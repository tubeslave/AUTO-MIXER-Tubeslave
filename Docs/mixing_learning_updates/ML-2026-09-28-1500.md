## Source Card

### `SRC-ML-20260928-1500-01` — Music Source Separation via Stem Discovery

- **Stable ID / source:** `arXiv:2609.30912v1`, DOI `10.48550/arXiv.2609.30912` — https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30912
- **Status/date:** ICASSP 2027 submission; submitted 25 September 2026; discovered 28 September 2026.
- **Reading depth:** complete five-page paper, including algorithm, training/evaluation sets, both tables, listening-test protocol, limitations and linked-artifact status.
- **Method:** a 37M-parameter diffusion-transformer Stem Embedding Generator recursively predicts one 128-dimensional PaSST/PCA query plus a residual embedding. A fixed stop-symbol cosine threshold `0.9` terminates recursion, with a hard cap of 16 iterations. Queries condition either a medium BS-Locoformer or a roughly 2.2B-parameter flow-matching DiT separator.
- **Data:** wet, processed stems from MoisesDB plus 125 MedleyDB and 10 AlbumDB songs; 279 songs train, 26 validation and 48 test. Tests contain 1,062 non-overlapping ten-second segments, averaging 4.5 active groups or 7.0 active instruments. Bleeding MoisesDB tracks are excluded.
- **Stem discovery:** SEG reaches group `F1=0.88`, precision `0.92`, recall `0.86`, count error `0.67`; instrument `F1=0.74`, precision `0.77`, recall `0.72`, count error `1.26`. These are segment means, and the label assignment itself is bounded by a separately trained classifier. Feeding the previous estimated residual worsens every reported discovery metric; the authors attribute this to train/inference exposure bias.
- **Separation scope:** generated versus oracle queries yield nearly unchanged Judge scores and at most `0.01` worse MERT-MSE on correctly detected sources. However, Table 2 excludes missed/mislabelled targets and retains only sources detected by all compared blind systems: 3,422 group stems and 5,197 instruments. It therefore does not measure end-to-end blind separation utility.
- **Metrics:** SAM-Audio Judge uses the known taxonomy label as text context; SNR penalizes phase differences; MERT-MSE is embedding-based. All three are computed on mono signals. They do not establish stereo phase, additive reconstruction, true-peak safety or mixing preference.
- **Listening:** 13 experienced participants, headphones in soundproof booths, four-second excerpts, two songs per each of five VDBGP instruments. Pairwise binomial tests show MuS3D/DiT-Sep beating HTDemucs and SAM-Audio and matching oracle-query DiT-Sep and autoencoded ground truth, while raw ground truth beats all autoencoded conditions. The small song set, selected correctly detected stems, lack of reported effect sizes/intervals and unreported multiplicity correction limit generalisation.
- **Artifact/compute boundary:** SEG trains on one H200; the DiT separator fine-tunes for about 300k iterations and uses 32 sampling steps. The exact repository is unavailable at discovery time, and the paper calls inference computationally expensive.
- **Limited conclusion:** generated audio-query embeddings can replace oracle queries without a measured quality loss *after correct detection* on the paper's selected ten-second mono evaluation. This does not validate unattended full-song stem discovery, causal/live operation, stereo reconstruction or automatic mix control.

### `EXP-ML-20260928-1500-01` — generated versus oracle query, same separator

- **Baseline A:** oracle stem embedding from the held-out target stem.
- **Variant B:** MuS3D-generated embedding matched to that target; query source is the only factor.
- **Controls:** identical checkpoint, mixture, target, hierarchy level, sampler/seed set, steps, codec, gain, segment and renderer.
- **Measure:** raw-output SNR/SDR with pinned definitions, MERT distance, additive residual, stereo phase/correlation, boundary continuity, sample/true peak and blind artifact/preference scores on separate BS.1770-matched randomized copies.
- **Protect:** source identity, attacks, pitch, ambience, stereo/mono, non-target leakage and headroom.
- **Success:** generated queries remain within preregistered equivalence bounds at song-grouped confidence intervals.
- **Cancel:** exact checkpoint/code is unavailable or query matching uses ground-truth labels differently between arms.

### `EXP-ML-20260928-1500-02` — independent segments versus whole-song query tracking

- **Baseline A:** discover queries independently in each ten-second segment.
- **Variant B:** carry/match query identity across segments; identity policy is the only factor.
- **Controls:** same checkpoint, audio, segment grid, source-count cap, stop threshold, sampler seeds, separator and reconstruction.
- **Measure:** stem swaps, duplicates/misses, embedding trajectory, boundary clicks, additive residual, per-section leakage, stereo/mono, sample/true peak and blind continuity on loudness-matched copies.
- **Protect:** instrument identity through entrances/exits, long tails, transitions, groove and headroom.
- **Cancel:** whole-song implementation changes separator weights or receives extra labels.

### `EXP-ML-20260928-1500-03` — parallel mid/high bass saturation

- **Baseline A:** complementary low and mid/high DI bands recombined clean.
- **Variant B:** identical split with one fixed saturation processor added only to a duplicate mid/high return; return is the sole change.
- **Controls:** same crossover, phase mode, latency compensation, low path, EQ, compression, faders, arrangement section and master chain.
- **Measure:** null/polarity and latency, low-band RMS, onset/crest, harmonic distribution, kick masking, guitar/bass separation, mono residual, sample/true peak; blind full-mix preference on BS.1770-matched randomized copies.
- **Protect:** fundamental, note definition, pick/finger attack, kick space, guitar edge, mono and headroom.
- **Cancel:** compensation fails, effect wins only by level, sub weakens or dense guitars become harsher.

### `EXP-ML-20260928-1500-04` — fundamental pre-emphasis into compression

- **Baseline A:** flat pre-EQ into the fixed compressor/channel chain.
- **Variant B:** one low-band boost near the measured fundamental before the same chain; pre-emphasis is the only factor.
- **Controls:** identical bass/amp balance, HPF, compressor threshold/ratio/attack/release, makeup gain, upper EQ, automation and master chain.
- **Measure:** compressor gain-reduction distribution, low-note variance, kick-band masking, sub translation, crest, sample/true peak and section-conditioned blind preference on loudness-matched copies.
- **Protect:** pitch, transient shape, kick separation, low-note equality, small-speaker audibility and headroom.
- **Cancel:** downstream GR is not held comparable, boost overloads the chain, low notes bloom or result wins only by level.
