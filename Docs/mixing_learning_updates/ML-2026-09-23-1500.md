## Search result

The 23 September 2026 arXiv Sound and Audio/Speech lists were checked against the current index by arXiv ID and normalized title. Two new, relevant preprints were found and read in full: [SRF-SVB](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.25610) for singing-editing safeguards and [GeoAtt HRTF upsampling](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.25995) for spatial-rendering evidence boundaries. SpanSynth-Edit and MambaVoice were excluded as duplicates of the 09:00/12:00 updates.

The main revision from SRF-SVB is that its inference is not an unguided automatic correction path. It requires a same-content professional performance: professional pitch guides the generated region and DTW between amateur and professional pitch curves supplies the time mapping. Its strongest timbre metric is also not independent because CAM++ supplies both the conditioning embedding and the SECS evaluation. The 15-listener, 30-sample MOS test supports a bounded research comparison, not automatic approval for rock vocals; on Chinese material SRF-SVB quality MOS overlaps NSVB and is numerically slightly lower.

GeoAtt improves held-out-listener LSD/ILD on SONICOM with one variable-context model, but it predicts only HRTF magnitude. Minimum phase and ITD are deferred to separate reconstruction/estimation, no localization or listening test is reported, and code/weights are promised only upon acceptance. Its numeric gains therefore remain benchmark evidence, not authorization to enable a binaural renderer.

## Source Cards

### `SRC-ML-20260923-1500-01` — SRF-SVB

- Title: *SRF-SVB: Style-Consistent Singing Voice Beautifying via Rectified Flow*.
- Stable ID: `arXiv:2609.25610v1`; detected 2026-09-23, submitted 2026-09-22.
- Reading depth: `full_text_complete`; methods, data, metrics, tables, ablation and conclusion read.
- Method: rectified-flow masked mel inpainting at 22.05 kHz; amateur timbre/content plus professional pitch and DTW-aligned timing; NSF-HiFiGAN vocoder; ten Euler steps.
- Evidence: about 160 hours of training data; 617 English and 874 Chinese paired test segments; 15 volunteers rated 30 clips. SRF-SVB improves reported pitch/timbre metrics and English MOS-Q, while Chinese MOS-Q is comparable to NSVB.
- Limits: professional same-content guidance at inference; CAM++ is shared by conditioning and the SECS metric; one Chinese test path uses UVR-extracted professional dry vocals; no rock-specific test, expert panel, stereo/phase/true-peak test, seed robustness or production-session evaluation. Pronunciation degradation is acknowledged.
- Status: `research_candidate`; not a pitch-correction preset or an automatic vocal rule.

### `SRC-ML-20260923-1500-02` — GeoAtt HRTF upsampling

- Title: *HRTF Upsampling Across Varying Measurement Configurations with Geometry-Aware Query-Conditioned Aggregation*.
- Stable ID: `arXiv:2609.25995v1`; detected 2026-09-23, submitted 2026-09-22.
- Reading depth: `full_text_complete`; formulation, architecture, loss, split, baselines, tables, ablations and conclusion read.
- Method: per-frequency query-conditioned aggregation with relative geometric bias, followed by frequency-axis Conformer modeling. Training uses SONICOM listeners 1–180; 20 listeners are held out.
- Evidence: one model has the lowest mean LSD across four LAP configurations and improves over SConvCNP on unseen structured measurement layouts. The strongest ablation effect comes from query conditioning; relative geometry adds a smaller consistent gain.
- Limits: magnitude-only prediction; minimum-phase reconstruction and ITD are outside the evaluated graph; LSD/ILD only; no localization, external dataset, listening test, phase/time-domain, mono or musical-mix evaluation. Code and weights were not public at reading time.
- Status: `research_candidate`; no renderer or spatial-mix rule is activated.

### `EXP-ML-20260923-1500-03` — GeoAtt magnitude stage versus complete binaural chain

- Baseline: measured held-out HRTF rendered end to end.
- Variant: upsampled magnitude plus explicitly pinned minimum-phase and ITD stages.
- Single factor: measured versus upsampled HRTF source.
- Fixed: listener, source material, directions, renderer, gain and headphone chain.
- Measurements: LSD/ILD, ITD error, impulse timing, interaural coherence, mono residual, true peak, localization/externalization and blind matched preference.
- Protect: direction, distance impression, tonal balance, transients and mono compatibility.
- Cancel: front/back confusion, localization spread, phase/mono damage, peak regression or no repeatable preference.

### `EXP-ML-20260923-1500-04` — query conditioning ablation

- Baseline: mean pooling over identical sparse HRTF measurements.
- Variant: query-conditioned aggregation without changing the frequency model.
- Single factor: spatial aggregation rule.
- Fixed: SONICOM split, measurement configuration, target directions, training budget and seeds.
- Measurements: per-listener/direction LSD and ILD, worst-case errors and seed dispersion; listening remains a separate gate.
- Protect: causal attribution and sparse-layout robustness.
- Cancel: gain disappears across seeds/listeners or is confined to aggregate averages.
