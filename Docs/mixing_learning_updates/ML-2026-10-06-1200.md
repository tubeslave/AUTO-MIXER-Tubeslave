### Method and data

The system freezes a 44.1 kHz, nine-quantizer DAC and predicts continuous PCA coordinates of the summed post-quantizer codec embedding. The native embedding width is 1,024; training-frame SVD gives a rank-72 structural subspace under the paper's tolerance. PCA was fitted only on 1,835,541 training frames. This 72-dimensional result is specific to the exact frozen DAC configuration and is not a universal codec or mixing dimension.

The symbolic interface uses eight drum families, 24 numeric channels plus articulation IDs, and a 250 Hz grid. Four context radii are 0, 22, 41 and 55 grid steps, corresponding to 0, 88, 164 and 220 ms. These are model receptive-field radii, not timing-edit ranges or recommended presets. A separate 60 ms boundary check is a dataset-window filter, not an editing threshold.

Evaluation uses non-overlapping four-beat windows derived from the Groove MIDI data lineage: 11,523 train, 1,534 validation and 1,733 test windows. Empty windows are removed. The paper says split definitions and artifacts are versioned, but public manifests were not available; independence at performer/session/source-performance level therefore could not be audited.

### Results and bounded interpretation

Selected table values:

| System | FAD-infinity | Mel error | Band-flux cosine | MRSTFT | Waveform L1 | RTF |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Direct PCA regressor | 0.355 | 13.04 | 0.836 | 0.136 | 0.0451 | 0.021 |
| Plain diffusion, 25 steps | 0.019 | 5.69 | 0.848 | 0.106 | 0.0536 | 0.077 |
| RVQ-CE diffusion, 6 steps | 0.022 | 5.47 | 0.866 | 0.105 | 0.0509 | 0.027 |

The 25-step plain diffusion improves paired mel error over the direct regressor by 7.35 dB, reported 95% CI [7.19, 7.51], and band-flux cosine by 0.012, CI [0.007, 0.017]. The six-step RVQ-CE variant improves over six-step plain diffusion by 0.92 dB mel error, CI [0.81, 1.03], and 0.023 band-flux cosine, CI [0.018, 0.029]. Tests use 2,000 paired bootstrap resamples, 2,000 paired sign-flip permutations and Holm correction within metric.

These are metric-specific gains. The direct regressor remains better on waveform L1, so diffusion is not a universal winner. Fifty steps are slower and do not add supported mel benefit over 25 steps. The paper's raw RMS metric has a gain-handling artifact even for reconstruction ceilings; the authors appropriately withhold RMS claims pending regeneration of a peak-normalized column.

FAD-infinity is distributional and unpaired. The symbolic nearest-neighbour baseline has a superficially low FAD-infinity of 0.025 while paired mel error is 17.54 and band-flux cosine 0.330. Therefore FAD alone cannot establish fidelity to the requested drum grid. Paired event, spectrum/transient and full-mix listening gates remain necessary.

### Limitations

- short four-beat windows rather than full songs or arrangement sections;
- explicit symbolic grid rather than a timbre-reference or multi-mic production interface;
- automatic metrics only; no listener study or user study;
- one generated sample per conditioning input, so bootstrap intervals do not include sampling-seed variability;
- no real rock multitrack, drum-room, phase/coherence, cymbal-tail or bass-pocket evaluation;
- metric disagreement between waveform and spectral/transient objectives;
- source/session independence cannot be independently audited without split manifests;
- the 72-dimensional subspace is tied to the exact DAC configuration;
- cleaned public code, split manifests, checkpoints and evaluation artifacts are described as a future release.

Confidence: medium for the reported internal protocol, low for direct production transfer.

### RULE-ML-20261005-1500-01 — preserve event expression in drum replacement/rendering

Do not convert the paper's 0/88/164/220 ms context radii or 60 ms boundary filter into edit ranges.
