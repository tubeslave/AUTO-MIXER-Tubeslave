## Source Card revisions

### SRC-ML-20261017-1500-02 — Diffusion warm initialization

- Stable ID: `conference:dafx:2026:DAFx26_paper_50`
- Canonical paper: https://dafx.de/paper-archive/2026/papers/DAFx26_paper_50.pdf
- arXiv lineage: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18968
- Authors: Cristóbal Andrade and Sebastian J. Schlecht
- Reading depth: `full_text_complete_8_pages`
- Status: `full_text_reviewed`
- Quantitative scope: two timbre-transfer studies, oboe-to-piano and string-to-clarinet. Each configuration uses 50 generated samples; FAD uses 100 unconditional target-instrument samples.
- Fixed reported setup: Stable Audio Open, 100 inference steps and guidance `omega=30` for transfer. The FAD reference set is generated at `omega=7`, so even `tau_init=0` retains a guidance mismatch.
- Metrics: pitch-set Jaccard distance derives pitch sets through mono MELODIA and discards note order, timing, duration and dynamics. FAD addresses distribution similarity but not preservation of groove, attacks, stereo image, source identity or arrangement.
- Evidence correction: the authors describe a useful region around `tau_init≈0.8`, `JD<0.6`, `FAD<0.7` using informal listening. The paper reports no listener count, screening, anchors, blinding, loudness matching, repeated-measures analysis or uncertainty for that preference.
- Noise finding: with `lambda=1`, pitch-set distance is consistently worse above roughly `tau_init=0.65`, while FAD is largely comparable; the authors attribute the loss of faithfulness to stronger hallucination. This is model- and source-dependent, not a universal rule.
- Application scope: MIDI-to-real and enhancement are demonstrated through companion examples, not a controlled quantitative benchmark. Voice guides are weaker, and unwanted sources—especially percussive/drum components—may persist or become artificial.
- Bounded conclusion: warm initialization is a research transformation control whose realism/faithfulness trade-off must be re-established for a pinned model, source class and seed block. It is not evidence for automatic cleanup, separation or mix improvement.
- Citation check: `partial`.
