## Literature and reproducible implementations

### SRC-ML-20260913-1500-01 — systematic review of stereo-image descriptions

- **Primary source:** Richard Mitic, Andreas Rossholm, *A systematic literature review of methods to describe the stereo image of recorded music*, published 2026-06-09, DOI `10.1186/s13636-026-00463-4`. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13636-026-00463-4
- **Reading depth:** full open-access HTML: methods, 178 included studies, 29 “very relevant” papers, cue taxonomy, stereo-image-profile framework, validation, datasets, limitations.
- **New project-relevant result:** the paper explicitly treats a stereo image profile as **context-specific**. Amplitude cues, ITD/IPD, correlation/coherence, M/S energy and higher-level summaries answer different questions; feature choice depends on the intended output and the input signal.
- **Material dependence:** the review notes that IACC measured in the same space changes with the musical material. Therefore correlation/coherence cannot be used as a universal monotonic width target.
- **Measurement caveat:** goniometer-derived width entangles amplitude and phase; a gain change can appear as a width change and vice versa.
- **Validation:** perceptual tests appeared in roughly half the reviewed studies. Objective descriptors remain task-linked diagnostics, not substitutes for a project listening test.
- **Limitations:** one researcher performed most data extraction; terminology across literature is inconsistent; search cut-off was 2026-02-26; the review gives no rock-guitar settings.
- **Confidence:** high for taxonomy/framework; medium-high for application to our rock-mix evaluator.

### SRC-ML-20260913-1500-02 — open-source, frequency-dependent stereo widener

- **Primary/official sources:** Orchisama Das, *An Open-Source Stereo Widening Plugin*, DAFx-24; official CC0 implementation: https://github.com/orchidas/StereoWidener ; proceedings PDF: https://github.com/IoSR-Surrey/DAFx24-Proceedings/blob/main/papers/DAFx24_paper_92.pdf
- **Reading depth:** paper abstract plus official implementation README and architecture description. The full paper body was not extracted, the plug-in was not built, and no audio was auditioned.
- **Architecture actually documented:** velvet-noise convolution or randomized all-pass decorrelation; perfect-reconstruction low/high filterbanks; independent low/high width controls; a transient-handling block intended to prevent decorrelator smearing.
- **Limited conclusion:** frequency-dependent width and attack protection are legitimate control dimensions to test separately. The design does not prove that any crossover or decorrelation amount is correct for our guitars.
- **Confidence:** medium-high for architecture, low until our controlled audio test.

### SRC-ML-20260913-1500-03 — DiffStereo

- **Primary source:** Suqi Zhang et al., *DiffStereo: End-to-End Mono-to-Stereo Audio Generation with Diffusion Transformer*, Interspeech 2025, DOI `10.21437/Interspeech.2025-1850`. https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2025/zhang25q_interspeech.pdf
- **Reading depth:** full 5-page paper.
- **Evaluation:** trained/tested on MUSDB18HQ; used Mid/Side and Left/Right LSD/SI-SNR/FAD plus normalized width, channel correlation and phase correlation. A 12-participant MOS study asked separately about naturalness, quality and spatialization; each participant heard five random sets.
- **Important result:** DiffStereo received the strongest spatialization score but lower naturalness and quality than both compared baselines. The authors explicitly identify a spatialization–naturalness tradeoff.
- **Level caveat:** the paper notes that higher frame-to-frame loudness may have contributed to preference, so loudness alignment remains mandatory.
- **Limits:** small subjective sample, short training segments, mono-to-stereo generation rather than mixing, and no evidence for safe use on a finished rock mix.
- **Confidence:** high for reported result; low for direct adoption.

### SRC-ML-20260913-1500-04 — phase-aware stereo music reconstruction

- **Primary source:** Kangdi Wang et al., *Back to Ear: Perceptually Driven High Fidelity Music Reconstruction*, arXiv:2509.14912v1, 2025-09-18. https://arxiv.org/html/2509.14912v1 ; official code: https://github.com/Eps-Acoustic-Revolution-Lab/EAR_VAE
- **Reading depth:** full HTML and linked implementation metadata.
- **Method:** magnitude supervision over Mid/Side/Left/Right; phase supervision restricted to Left/Right; additional losses on instantaneous frequency and group delay; custom individual- and cross-channel phase-coherence metrics.
- **Useful boundary:** spatial magnitude, LR phase and transient/phase derivatives are separate validation axes. A good spectral reconstruction alone does not establish correct stereo phase or attack.
- **Limits:** preprint; about 10,000 hours of proprietary in-house training audio; training used eight A100 GPUs; only objective evaluations and spectrogram ablations were reported, without a formal listening test.
- **Confidence:** medium for the proposed diagnostics, low for perceptual superiority.

## What is genuinely new

1. A global correlation or width score is not a scientifically defensible optimization target for rhythm-guitar width. The required stereo descriptor must be chosen for the task, frequency region and section.
2. Frequency-dependent widening and transient protection should be separate candidate controls. This adds a safer experiment axis beyond the already-known rejection of a delayed-copy “double”.
3. “Wider” and “better” are separate judgments. DiffStereo’s small listening test found the widest/most spatial result less natural and lower quality.
4. AI stereo validation should separate M/S magnitude, L/R phase, phase derivatives/transients and perceptual listening. None of the new metrics becomes an automatic acceptance gate.

## Candidate rules

### RULE-ML-20260913-1500-01 — build a task-specific stereo panel

- **Source:** Mitic & Rossholm 2026.
- **When:** evaluating rhythm-guitar width or reference spatial matching.
- **Change:** select amplitude, phase/coherence, M/S and mono checks appropriate to the task; compute by frequency region and section.
- **Suggested range:** no fixed numeric target; compare candidates relative to the same input/reference. Origin: review synthesis plus project test design.
- **Do not damage:** center stability, guitar body, pick attack, kick–bass foundation, mono translation.
- **Verify:** loudness-matched blind A/B on verse and chorus plus bandwise diagnostics.
- **Undo when:** metrics disagree with stable blind preference or encourage artifacts.
- **Confidence:** high; `auto_apply:false`.

### RULE-ML-20260913-1500-02 — correlation is descriptive, not a target

- **Source:** Mitic & Rossholm 2026.
- **When:** an optimizer tries to lower L/R correlation to produce width.
- **Change:** use correlation/coherence only as a material-dependent diagnostic.
- **Suggested range:** none. Origin: systematic review.
- **Do not damage:** naturalness, localization, mono, transients.
- **Verify:** blind preference, mono fold-down and time-frequency cue inspection.
- **Undo when:** a validated task-specific mapping exists for the exact material class.
- **Confidence:** high; `auto_apply:false`.

### RULE-ML-20260913-1500-03 — protect guitar attacks during decorrelation

- **Source:** Das, DAFx-24 implementation architecture.
- **When:** decorrelation broadens sustained guitar body but blurs pick/palm-mute attacks.
- **Change:** compare transient-preserving or attack-bypassed decorrelation with the untreated and unprotected variants.
- **Suggested range:** detector timing must be derived from performance; no source-backed universal milliseconds. Origin: architecture plus test proposal.
- **Do not damage:** pick definition, palm-mute punch, groove, phase stability.
- **Verify:** matched-loudness transient A/B, envelope inspection and mono.
- **Undo when:** protection causes pumping, width modulation or audible switching.
- **Confidence:** medium-high; `auto_apply:false`.

### RULE-ML-20260913-1500-04 — separate low- and high-band width candidates

- **Source:** Das, DAFx-24 implementation architecture.
- **When:** broadband widening weakens the central low-frequency foundation.
- **Change:** test independent low/high width rather than globally narrowing the instrument.
- **Suggested range:** choose crossover from inspected guitar/kick/bass overlap; no fixed frequency copied. Origin: architecture plus project test design.
- **Do not damage:** kick–bass solidity, guitar weight, crossover continuity, mono.
- **Verify:** band-limited and full-mix A/B in stereo and mono.
- **Undo when:** crossover coloration disconnects guitar body from edge.
- **Confidence:** medium-high; `auto_apply:false`.

### RULE-ML-20260913-1500-05 — rate width, naturalness and quality separately

- **Source:** DiffStereo subjective test.
- **When:** selecting generated or algorithmically widened stereo.
- **Change:** ask separate blind questions for spatialization, naturalness and overall quality.
- **Suggested range:** none. Origin: reported MOS design.
- **Do not damage:** timbre, plausible image, center, fatigue.
- **Verify:** loudness-matched blind ratings; include unprocessed/reference anchors.
- **Undo when:** a deliberate artificial effect is the explicit creative goal.
- **Confidence:** high; `auto_apply:false`.

### RULE-ML-20260913-1500-06 — separate M/S magnitude and L/R phase validation

- **Source:** systematic review, DiffStereo and epsilon-ar-VAE.
- **When:** validating AI stereo reconstruction or mono-to-stereo proposals.
- **Change:** report M/S and L/R spectral/phase behavior separately; add listening and mono checks.
- **Suggested range:** reference-relative deltas, no global thresholds. Origin: source evaluation designs.
- **Do not damage:** transients, side detail, inter-channel phase, true peak, mono.
- **Verify:** held-out audio, independent metrics and loudness-matched blind A/B.
- **Undo when:** the system is not reconstructing or generating stereo audio.
- **Confidence:** medium-high; `auto_apply:false`.

### RULE-ML-20260913-1500-07 — audit learned representations for phase/transient fidelity

- **Source:** epsilon-ar-VAE.
- **When:** a learned representation feeds spatial or mixing decisions.
- **Change:** test phase-derivative and cross-channel preservation before trusting latent features.
- **Suggested range:** no thresholds until calibrated on project listening data. Origin: paper method plus conservative project policy.
- **Do not damage:** attack clarity, inter-channel cues, high-frequency detail, stereo position.
- **Verify:** null/delta renders, ICPC/CCPC-style diagnostics and blind listening.
- **Undo when:** metrics fail to predict audible damage on held-out rock material.
- **Confidence:** medium; `auto_apply:false`.
