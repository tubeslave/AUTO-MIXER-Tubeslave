### `EXP-ML-20260929-0900-01` — rhythm support under a lead-guitar entrance

- **Status:** `not_run`.
- **Baseline A:** current approved balance and processing through a section where lead guitar enters.
- **Variant B:** automate only the rhythm-guitar group level by `-1.0 dB` during the lead phrase; the value is a project-designed probe, not a source-derived preset.
- **Controls:** same edits, pans, EQ, compression, ambience, lead level, vocal treatment and mix-bus chain. Render raw copies for peak/safety checks and separate BS.1770-matched randomized copies for listening.
- **Measure:** section loudness, lead-to-rhythm level relation, guitar-bus crest/onset statistics, sample/true peak, mono compatibility, and Dmitry's blind ratings for lead audibility, rhythm weight, chorus energy and naturalness.
- **Protect:** groove, rhythm-wall drive, lead phrasing, vocal role, stereo anchor and headroom.
- **Success:** lead phrases become easier to follow without a reliable loss of rhythm weight or section energy.
- **Cancel:** B wins only because of overall level, the rhythm bed collapses, the lead becomes detached, or the arrangement's intended guitar hierarchy is weakened.
