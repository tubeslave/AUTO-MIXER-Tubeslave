## New Source Card

### SRC-ML-20261006-0900-01 — Bleed No More

- title: Bleed No More: Generative Interference Reduction for Musical Recordings
- authors: Rajesh R, Rashen Fernando, Padmanabhan Rajan, Ryan M. Corey
- venue: ICASSP 2026
- DOI: 10.1109/ICASSP55912.2026.11460429
- full legal PDF: https://corey1.web.engr.illinois.edu/download/rajesh_icassp2026_bleed.pdf
- demo: https://listeningtech.github.io/cGANIR/
- reading depth: complete five-page paper — method, datasets, tables, ablation, conclusion and limitations
- status: studied_full_text_peer_reviewed
- citation_check: partial

The system is a conditional WGAN for single-channel bleed reduction: a U-Net magnitude-spectrogram generator, PatchGAN critic, WGAN-GP plus L1 loss, and mixture phase for reconstruction. Training used 7,560 simulated MUSDB18HQ pairs plus 2,300 Sanidha pairs; mono 22.05 kHz audio, 2,047-sample Hann STFT, 512-sample hop, 10-second segments with 50% overlap. Re-recorded MUSDB18HQ and live Saraga examples were also evaluated, but Saraga was qualitative only.

Selected drum results show the central trade-off. On simulated drums, cWGAN-IR raised SI-SDR from 13.09 to 13.87 dB and SIR from 25.36 to 29.72 dB versus HTDemucs, while SAR fell from 20.77 to 15.60 dB. On re-recorded drums, SI-SDR improved from -8.58 to -2.58 dB and SIR from 18.39 to 18.79 dB, while SAR fell from 5.06 to -0.90 dB.

Bounded conclusion: reported isolation can improve while artifact/fidelity risk worsens. SI-SDR or SIR alone cannot support a production rule. The paper has no formal listener test, no real rock multitrack evaluation, no multi-mic coherence test, and no ghost-note, transient, pocket or full-mix preference analysis. Its advertised GitHub repository returned 404 at check time, so independent reproduction is not established.

Confidence: medium for the reported protocol; low for direct rock-mix transfer.
