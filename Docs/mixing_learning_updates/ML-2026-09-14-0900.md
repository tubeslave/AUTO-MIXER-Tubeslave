## What is genuinely new

The previous dynamics package had Thompson and Heilemann only at abstract depth. The full arXiv text is now readable, so this update upgrades that card instead of pretending the paper is a new discovery.

Its most useful result is methodological: two compressor models can look similarly accurate in waveform L1 or multi-resolution short-time energy while producing meaningfully different gain-reduction trajectories. In the authors' ten single-example fits, the largest-to-smallest error spread was 85.9% under direct GR error, but only 2.8% under waveform L1 and 13.3% under MSTE. This does **not** prove which version sounds better: there was no listening test and no validation split.

The resulting project rule is to keep three score families separate:

1. **Behavior:** static curve, response-defined threshold and time-aligned GR trajectory.
2. **Color:** waveform/spectral delta, phase, noise and nonlinear coloration.
3. **Preference:** loudness-matched blind judgment in the complete rock arrangement.

Do not collapse these into one score unless the weighting is explicit and a ranking-sensitivity check shows that the winner is stable.

## Sources actually read

### 1. Thompson & Heilemann — control-voltage evaluation

- Primary source: [full arXiv text](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.18573v1), arXiv:2606.18573, 17 June 2026.
- Reading depth: methods, equations, experimental results, dataset construction, alignment and limitations.
- Upgrade: `SRC-DYN-20260913-201140-05` was `abstract_only`; the new card `SRC-ML-20260914-0900-01` records the full-text reading.
- Limited conclusion: when a trustworthy GR/control trajectory is available, it is the clearest behavioral reference. Waveform and energy metrics remain useful for output similarity, but they are not equivalent to GR error.
- Important boundary: ten models were fitted to ten individual 30-second examples and evaluated on those same examples. The tested topology could not reproduce lower-ratio SSL curvature or auto release, so training was restricted to 10:1 and fixed release settings.

The paper also gives a reproducible measurement contract:

- a 1 kHz quasi-static ramp from −48 to 0 dB at 1.6 dB/s for the response curve;
- multiple-duration bursts for attack, release and programme dependence;
- eleven steady bursts from 4 to 14 dB above threshold for CV scaling;
- a below-threshold maximum-length sequence for input/output alignment;
- real programme material after the calibration probes.

These are **test stimuli from the study**, not recommended musical settings. The dataset's CV WAV files are control data with large DC components and must never be routed to monitors.

### 2. Sun, Fourer & Maaref — blind DRC identification/inversion

- Primary source: [full arXiv text](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.19645v1), arXiv:2607.19645, dated 24 August 2026.
- Reading depth: formulation, features, optimization, results, ablation and conclusion.
- Limited conclusion: amplitude-distribution features can reveal compression-related redistribution missed by a single peak/RMS number. Parameter accuracy and reconstruction quality are different objectives.
- Boundary: 25 mono excerpts, five per genre, synthetic compression/expansion from one model family, no listening test. A single dry centroid per effect type erases instrument, articulation and section roles. Reported optimization took 124–530 seconds per 30-second excerpt, so this is not a live Automixer path.

### 3. Gu et al. — Solid State Bus-Comp

- Primary source: [full arXiv text](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.04589v2), arXiv:2504.04589, 21 May 2025; [project page](https://www.yichenggu.com/SolidStateBusComp/).
- Reading depth: dataset construction, statistics, benchmark tables, split, ablation and limitations.
- Limited conclusion: compressor-model validation should hold out both songs and control settings. The benchmark uses 112 training and 63 test songs, with 76 parameter combinations reserved as unseen.
- Boundary: the benchmark uses waveform L1 and M-STFT, has no listening test and was trained for 500,000 steps on an H200. The dataset/project page does not state unambiguous audio redistribution terms in the material read; no download or Reference Card was added. The paper reports 2528.53 hours, while the project page says 2258 hours; the discrepancy is preserved.

### 4. AQFeatures open implementation

- Reproducible source: [dfourer/AQFeatures](https://github.com/dfourer/AQFeatures).
- Reading depth: README, CC BY-NC 4.0 license and static inspection of `start_process_db.m`.
- Limited conclusion: paired original/processed signals and a profile-variance to programme-variance feature criterion provide a useful descriptor-sensitivity test.
- Boundary: the repository warns it may not exactly reproduce the cited research; code was not run, and its non-commercial license blocks unrestricted production reuse.
