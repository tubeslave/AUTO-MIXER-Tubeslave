### `SRC-ML-20260922-0900-01` — fast direction-dependent reverberation

- Primary source: [arXiv full HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.24809), `arXiv:2609.24809v1`, 21 September 2026, CC BY 4.0; the manuscript states acceptance to the 161st AES Convention.
- Reading depth: `full_text_complete`, including algorithms, complexity analysis, filter design, experiments, conclusion and appendix.
- Method: time-varying exponentiated convolution; recursive alternative; spherical-harmonic linear-transform extension; smooth near-minimum-phase filters fitted to direction/frequency-dependent `T60` fields drawn from non-stationary Gaussian processes.
- Reproducible details, not presets: MATLAB/Mac M1 timing tests averaged over 20 runs at 48 kHz; demonstration-specific filter lengths, grid sizes, `T60` values and GP hyperparameters.
- Result: the optimized exponentiating method reduces direct `O(M²N)` work to a partitioned FFT formulation with stated log-linear factors; the recursive formulation adds an `N²` factor. Short filters amplify fitting error under exponentiation. Reducing a demonstrated spherical grid from 25 to 16 points introduced high-frequency spatial aliasing for short directional decay.
- Limits: synthetic Gaussian fields and simulated pressure responses; no real-room generalization, controlled listening, localization, early-reflection identity, direct/reverberant balance, mono, decoder/loudspeaker, sample/true-peak or mix-context evaluation.
