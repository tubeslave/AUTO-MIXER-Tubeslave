## Result

The full eight-page DAFx-20 paper *Virtual Bass System With Fuzzy Separation of Tones and Transients* was reviewed together with its official companion page and public MATLAB repository. The source supports a narrow playback-path claim: harmonic generation can improve perceived bass when a small loudspeaker removes low frequencies, but it does not establish that a distorted bass layer improves a full-range rock mix. The full-band reference remained far ahead of every virtual-bass method in the authors' MUSHRA-style test (mean 98.6 versus 47.0 for the proposed method).

No audio was downloaded or processed. Commercial excerpts listed by the companion repository were not treated as legal project multitracks or copied into the knowledge base.

- method: low-passed content downsampled to 4096 Hz; 512-sample Hann STFT with hop 64; fuzzy tonal/transient/noise separation; nonlinear distortion for transients; phase-vocoder harmonic generation for tonal content; noise left unprocessed.
- evaluation: four approximately 11-second commercial excerpts, proposed method plus three comparators, full-band hidden reference and a 150 Hz high-pass anchor; 18 participants recruited, 7 excluded, 11 analysed; headphone models were uncontrolled; parameters were tuned by ear per stimulus.
- main_result: proposed method ranked above the other virtual-bass systems on average, but the full-band reference remained much preferred; several per-stimulus differences had overlapping confidence intervals.
- limitations: small analysed sample; uncontrolled headphones; manual per-stimulus tuning; commercial excerpts; no rock-mix workflow comparison; no evidence for full-range artistic bass distortion; no independent replication located in this run.
- bounded_conclusion: use as evidence for constrained-playback virtual bass only, not as a universal bass-mixing rule.

## Knowledge Cards

### KC-ML-20261012-1200-01 — Playback-path condition

Virtual bass is a delivery/playback intervention for systems that cannot reproduce the fundamental. It must not be relabelled as low-frequency recovery or generalized into a full-range mix-wide distortion rule.

### KC-ML-20261012-1200-02 — Reference ceiling

The proposed method's average score of 47.0 exceeded the tested alternatives but remained far below the full-band reference's 98.6. Relative improvement over a damaged anchor is not equivalence to intact bass reproduction.

### KC-ML-20261012-1200-03 — Programme dependence

Results varied by excerpt and parameters were tuned by ear per stimulus. No single cutoff, harmonic count, drive gain, slope or inharmonicity tolerance should be transferred as a rock preset.

### KC-ML-20261012-1200-04 — Live-deployment boundary

The public fuzzy-separation path is noncausal and approximately 150 ms delayed according to the author. It is unsuitable as a current Wing/live candidate without a separate causal redesign and target-host timing audit.

### KC-ML-20261012-1200-05 — Public code is not reproduction

Repository availability does not establish reproducibility or licence permission. Paper/script parameter drift, missing frozen stimulus manifests and unpinned MATLAB dependencies keep the artifact blocked.
