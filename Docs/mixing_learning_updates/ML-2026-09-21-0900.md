## Literature and implementation sources actually read

### SRC-ML-20260921-0900-01 — Open-Amp

- Primary text: [Open-Amp: Synthetic Data Framework for Audio Effect Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.14972), arXiv `2411.14972`, DOI `10.48550/arXiv.2411.14972`, ICASSP 2025.
- Reading depth: `full_text_complete`.
- Method: 59 amplifier and 101 pedal captures, a contrastive effects encoder, and a conditioned one-to-many TCN trained from online synthetic rendering. The paper reports cross-dataset representation results and low-data embedding-only enrolment for three analogue pedals.
- Result retained: greater device diversity improved the tested effect representation and sometimes made low-data enrolment competitive with training from scratch.
- Result bounded: every tested one-to-one device model outperformed the one-to-many model for its own device; the paper reports up to roughly 5 dB ESR loss for the universal model on the selected devices. There is no controlled listening test, and classification/reconstruction metrics do not prove a preferred rock-guitar sound.
- Numbers such as five conditioning positions, 3 s of enrolment audio, or the reported ESR gap are study conditions, not transferable presets.

### AUD-ML-20260921-0900-01 — Open-Amp repository

- Repository: [Alec-Wright/OpenAmp](https://github.com/Alec-Wright/OpenAmp), revision `b57993408a61d94bf9425ca0acf7af4b3c27f3eb`.
- Reading depth: `complete_tree_and_relevant_training_inference_static_audit`; execution: `false`.
- The public training script creates a `test_devs` list but validates on `train_devs`; it therefore does not demonstrate unseen-device validation. A newer seeded split helper exists elsewhere, but the main demo path does not use it for the reported validation.
- Dependencies are not version-pinned, the demo is described as a basic implementation rather than the exact paper configuration, and no root `LICENSE`/`COPYING` file was found.
- Rights are separable: the paper licence, repository code, crowd-sourced GuitarML models and IDMT audio do not share one blanket permission. The official IDMT-SMT-Guitar record is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0/evaluation-oriented; integration remains blocked pending artifact-specific rights review.

### SRC-ML-20260921-0900-02 — GOAT

- Primary text: [GOAT: A Large Dataset of Paired Guitar Audio Recordings and Tablatures](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.22655v1), arXiv `2509.22655`, DOI `10.48550/arXiv.2509.22655`, ISMIR 2025.
- Dataset record: [Zenodo 15690894](https://zenodo.org/records/15690894); code: [JackJamesLoth/GOAT-Dataset](https://github.com/JackJamesLoth/GOAT-Dataset).
- Reading depth: `full_text_complete` plus official repository audit.
- Useful structure: paired DI, tablature/aligned symbolic data and multiple re-amped variants, including many power-chord examples relevant to rock and metal evaluation.
- Result retained: amp-domain augmentation improved the paper's electric-guitar amp-domain transcription result relative to DI-only training, but it did not uniformly win on DI or GuitarSet, and the larger augmented condition was not uniformly better than the smaller one.
- Failed-result retained: token accuracy for the DadaGP/Whisper experiment was dominated by wait tokens and did not track musically correct sequences.
- Rights limitation: distribution is for research purposes by request, some performances are covers, and the authors state that they do not exclusively own all copyrights. GOAT is therefore a restricted research candidate, not a project Reference Card or licensed multitrack.
