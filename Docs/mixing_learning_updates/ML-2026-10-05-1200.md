### `SRC-ML-20261005-1200-01` — Deep Regularized RNNs for Virtual Analog Modeling

- stable ID: `arxiv:2509.15622`; conference alias `dafx:2026:DAFx26_paper_26`
- URLs: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.15622 and https://www.dafx.de/paper-archive/2026/papers/DAFx26_paper_26.pdf
- publication/version: arXiv v1 `2025-09-19`, v2 `2026-06-24`; DAFx-26 version family
- reading depth: full eight-page paper, equations/method, datasets, evaluation, tables, limitations and artifact links
- method: concatenation-conditioned deep LSTM; `L∞` or empirical-spectral-norm `L2` stability regularisation; 32-channel ERB-spaced gammatone-filterbank loss from 100 Hz to about 10 kHz plus residual
- material: ProCo Rat, Darkglass Duality Fuzz and Boss CS-3; one-second guitar/bass clips at 48 kHz
- key limitation: train/validation split only, with validation reused for testing; time-varying control trajectories were absent from training
- control-noise test: zero audio after impulse/state warm-up, then smoothed controls; it is not a non-zero-program automation test
- long-run comparison: `1×64`, `4×8`, `4×64`, three seeds, but table reports the best run rather than mean/dispersion
- bounded result: regularised models reduced the paper's zero-input control-noise measure, with a modelling-accuracy cost; `L2` generally narrowed that cost relative to `L∞`
- efficiency observation: regularised `4×8` outperformed regularised `1×64` in the reported metrics with roughly one order of magnitude fewer parameters/FLOPS
- absent: formal listening test, independent held-out set, non-zero-audio automation stress test, target-host callback/xrun benchmark, dry/wet phase test
- confidence: moderate for the need to test automation separately; low for direct architecture or production-setting transfer

Version correction: arXiv v1 explicitly reports mathematical mistakes in Eqs. 17 and 21; v2 expands the architecture/results. Conclusions are tied to v2/DAFx and do not count v1 plus DAFx as separate evidence.
