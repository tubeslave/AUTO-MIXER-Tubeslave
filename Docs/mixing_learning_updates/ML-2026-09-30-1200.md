## Scientific source-card upgrades

### SRC-ML-20260930-0900-01 — SAGE: Semantic Audio Generative Encoder

- **Stable ID:** `arXiv:2609.32755`; DOI `10.48550/arXiv.2609.32755`
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32755
- **Reading depth:** `full_text_method_main_results_listening_and_relevant_ablations`; paper licence `CC BY 4.0`.
- **Method read:** a 105M-parameter stereo complex-STFT VAE at 44.1 kHz, window 2048/hop 512, with x64 latent compression. Training combines complex-STFT, multi-resolution mel, explicit sum/difference, KL, semantic-distillation, adversarial and feature-matching terms. The frozen CLAP teacher is introduced after a detached warm-up.
- **Main results read:** SAGE leads 23/25 reported perceptual/distributional measurements across five evaluation sets, but SAME-L retains much higher SDR on MoisesDB mixtures (9.49 versus 4.79 dB) and stems (12.89 versus 6.69 dB). On MoisesDB mixtures SAME-L also has the better PANN-space FAD. This is metric disagreement, not a single overall winner.
- **Listening protocol read:** ten held-out 10-second commercial-music excerpts, hidden reference, 3.5-kHz low-pass anchor, four tested systems and reference-screening. With 21 retained raters, SAGE (81.6) and SAME-L (81.8) have overlapping 95% confidence intervals and are not separated by the test; both form a higher group than Stable Audio Open and CoDiCodec.
- **Stereo ablation read:** removing the sum/difference loss leaves mid SI-SDR almost unchanged but produces very weak/incorrect side reconstruction (reported side level around -12 dB and side SI-SDR around -14 dB). With the loss, side level is near the reference and width error falls. This supports an explicit stereo objective for this model family, not a target side level for a mix.
- **Semantic ablation read:** stronger CLAP distillation improves probing but can worsen FAD and SI-SDR. CLAP-based metrics moved against the CLAP-distilled model in the reported sweep, so simple teacher-metric inflation was not observed there; independence is still incomplete because the study and model family share representations and authors.
- **Compute/reproducibility:** paper reports 1536 + 3043 A100 GPU-hours for the two training stages. The linked GitHub repository `francescobrigante/SAGE` returned `404` at review time, so the claimed code/weights/evaluation harness could not be verified or licensed. Training was not attempted.
- **Project limit:** codec/representation evidence only. It does not show better balance, masking control, loudness, transients or musical decisions in rock mixes.

### SRC-ML-20260930-0900-02 — YuE2: targeted MERT2/SheetSage2 review

- **Stable ID:** `arXiv:2609.33757`; DOI `10.48550/arXiv.2609.33757`
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33757
- **Reading depth:** `targeted_full_text_sections_MERT2_SheetSage2_evaluation_appendix_protocol`; generation-quality sections were not exhaustively audited.
- **MERT2 method read:** 24-kHz mono log-mel input, ConvNeXt frontend and 24-layer 1024-dimensional Conformer at 25 Hz. Foundation pretraining predicts four cached code streams derived jointly from two teacher spaces. Separate full-song and causal/tokenizer branches are then adapted; the deployed semantic tokenizer uses a 32-dimensional, 32,768-entry quantizer at 25 Hz (375 bit/s).
- **MERT2 results read:** MERT2-30s exceeds the listed external baselines on 14/15 MARBLE metrics and the full-song branch on 13/15. The official card explicitly says baseline values were reproduced from another paper rather than rerun and protocols may differ. The claim is therefore useful screening evidence, not an independent universal benchmark victory.
- **SheetSage2 method read:** a frozen MERT2-FS backbone with trainable low-rank adapters and a six-layer autoregressive decoder emits melody, chords, key, beat/downbeat, meter and structure in one chronological sequence. Training labels are generated by SheetSage2-Prober from mixed human annotations, MIDI-rendered audio and then a large real-audio corpus; SheetSage2-AR is trained on 441,094 generated-label recordings (28,434 h).
- **SheetSage2 results read:** highest point estimate on 12/15 benchmark-metric pairs, but the official card warns that bold values are point estimates, not statistical significance. It does not lead GTZAN beat F1, HarmonixSet structure F1 at 0.5 s, or Chords1217 chord recall. Some comparator protocols differ, including cross-validation versus one fixed checkpoint and known training overlap for one madmom case.
- **Artifact/licence audit:** official Hugging Face cards are currently accessible for `m-a-p/MERT-v2-30s`, `m-a-p/MERT-v2-FullSong` and `m-a-p/SheetSage2`; all list `CC BY-NC 4.0` weights. SheetSage2 requires access/sign-in and `trust_remote_code`, and its card records chord-spelling fixes dated 2026-09-21 and 2026-09-24. Exact revision, dependency hashes and code review are required before any local use.
- **Project limit:** the encoders consume 24-kHz mono and their tasks concern music understanding/transcription, not stereo image, full-band mastering, balance quality or listener preference. They may propose annotations for review; they cannot replace calibrated audio measurements or section-role judgement.

## Knowledge Cards

### KC-ML-20260930-1200-01 — Codec quality is multidimensional

SAGE and SAME-L reverse order across learned/perceptual metrics and sample-aligned fidelity, while listeners do not distinguish them in the reported MUSHRA. Report distributional, waveform, stereo and listening endpoints separately; never collapse them into one “quality” score.

### KC-ML-20260930-1200-02 — Explicit stereo objectives can matter without changing the mid channel

SAGE's ablation shows a large side-channel failure despite similar mid SI-SDR when its sum/difference loss is removed. For stereo learned processing, inspect mid and side separately by band and section. The reported dB values remain source-specific diagnostics.

### KC-ML-20260930-1200-03 — Auto-generated labels are not independent ground truth

SheetSage2-AR can outperform its Prober teacher on several benchmarks, but its large-scale supervision is generated by that same Prober. Benchmark gains support the released transcription system; they do not independently validate every generated training label or downstream mix decision.

### KC-ML-20260930-1200-04 — Mono music embeddings have a hard stereo/full-band boundary

MERT2's 24-kHz mono preprocessing discards stereo cues and content above 12 kHz. Its embeddings may describe musical content, but cannot certify width, phase/mono safety, air-band balance or mastering quality.

### KC-ML-20260930-1200-05 — Model-card availability is part of reproducibility

A paper link, live card or claimed open release is insufficient. Pin exact revisions, verify weight/code licences separately, review remote code and record fixes that change outputs before planning an experiment.
