### SRC-ML-20261011-1200-01 — Inaugural MSR Challenge summary

- **Stable IDs:** `arxiv:2601.04343v1`; `doi:10.48550/arXiv.2601.04343`.
- **Publication:** arXiv v1, 2026-01-07; ICASSP 2026 challenge paper.
- **Reading depth:** complete HTML, including data split, metrics, team results, system summaries and discussion.
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/html/2601.04343v1

Objective evaluation used a 1,000-clip non-blind set with clean references. Subjective evaluation used a different 500-clip blind set of historical cylinders, live recordings, FM and low-bitrate streaming. Five systems were ranked. The paper reports `Spearman rho=0.9` because the two five-system orderings differ only by swapping AC_DC and Hachimi.

That rank agreement is not a validation of MMSNR, Zimtohrli or FAD at clip, stem or project level. An exact permutation calculation on the reported five ranks gives two-sided `p=0.0833` (10 of 120 permutations with `|rho| >= 0.9`; derived here, not reported by the paper). The paper also gives no listener count, confidence intervals, randomization/blinding detail or per-condition uncertainty in the visible full text. The morning statement is therefore corrected: `rho=0.9` is weak system-order evidence only and cannot replace Dmitry's project-multitrack listening gate.

The challenge results also show source dependence: percussion averaged 0.29 dB MMSNR across five systems versus 4.59 dB for bass. The authors attribute transient-rich percussion difficulty to phase reconstruction. This is a warning against transferring bass/tonal-source results to drums.

### SRC-ML-20261011-1200-02 — MSRBench

- **Stable IDs:** `arxiv:2510.10995v1`; `doi:10.48550/arXiv.2510.10995`.
- **Publication:** arXiv v1, 2025-10-13.
- **Reading depth:** complete HTML, including professional mix construction, degradation recipes, metrics, baselines, results and discussion.
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/html/2510.10995v1
- **Licence noted by source:** benchmark data CC-BY-NC; baseline implementation MIT.

MSRBench contains 2,000 ten-second 48 kHz stereo clips, eight source classes and 13 conditions. Engineers retain unprocessed dry references, processed stems and the mastered mixture. The production chain explicitly includes per-stem processing, gain/pan, bus compression/EQ/shared effects, and mastering. Twelve additional degradations then cover analog/acoustic conditions and codecs.

This clarifies the target contract. A model may be asked to infer dry references from a chain that includes non-invertible and shared processing. The paper itself describes restoration as increasingly generative as information is destroyed. Therefore a plausible output is not evidence that the actual original stem was recovered.

MSRBench's SI-SNR/FAD disagreement is informative but not a reason to discard phase-sensitive checks. The paper attributes very poor SI-SNR partly to phase mismatch and says listening sounded better, yet the visible text does not document that listening protocol. For Mixing Learning, phase/alignment, reconstruction residual and listening remain separate gates.

## Revised candidate rule

### RULE-ML-20261011-0900-01 — restoration outputs remain shadow evidence

- **When:** evaluating a preconditioner, separator or restorer on project audio.
- **Change:** declare the target explicitly as either dry captured tracks, processed group stems, or final rendered mix; never collapse these into “the original.”
- **Range / origin:** no transferable restoration amount; only on/off topology comparisons with a version-pinned model.
- **Protect:** source identity, attacks, phase, bleed/room, low-end timing, stereo/mono, and reconstruction coherence.
- **Verify:** one source class and degradation at a time; raw reference metrics plus separate BS.1770-matched blind listening; report per-stem results and failures; Dmitry verdict.
- **Cancel:** ambiguous target, unpinned code/weights/config, phase or attack damage, leakage, hallucinated content, or metric/listening disagreement.
- **Confidence:** medium.
- **`auto_apply`:** `false`.

## Revised multitrack experiment

### EXP-ML-20261011-0900-01 — Stage-1 preconditioner on `Пена`

- **Status:** `not_run_blocked`.
- **Ground truth:** start from the 13 already verified synchronous processed stems that reconstruct the `Пена` premaster. Freeze and publish a deterministic 13-to-8 source-class mapping; require the grouped sum to null against the premaster within the existing rounding tolerance before testing.
- **Input construction:** apply one frozen, declared mixture degradation to that exact premaster. First proposed condition is MP3 128 kbps because it is an MSRBench condition; the bitrate is source-derived, not a production recommendation.
- **A:** degraded premaster -> exact fixed separator.
- **B:** the same degraded premaster -> exact Stage-1 mixture restorer -> the same fixed separator.
- **Single factor:** Stage-1 enabled/disabled.
- **Target:** the eight grouped **processed** stems, not the 48 raw mic files. This isolates input-degradation restoration from de-mixing and avoids pretending that Stage 1 de-masters the production.
- **Frozen:** stem mapping, sum, excerpt, encoder/version/options, sample rate, separator checkpoint/config, seed, chunk/overlap and output gain.
- **Raw checks:** sample alignment, per-stem MMSNR and SI-SDR with interpretation limits, spectral/transient error, LR phase, leakage, reconstruction residual, clipping and true peak. FAD is excluded as a decisive per-clip metric because it is distributional.
- **Listening copies:** re-render A and B through the same frozen stem-to-mix map; create separate BS.1770-matched copies; three randomized blind trials by Dmitry, plus solo checks for artifacts.
- **Protected qualities:** drum attacks, vocal/instrument identity, room and bleed, bass timing, stereo/mono, premaster balance and summed coherence.
- **Success:** predeclared reference metrics improve without a protected regression and the same variant wins at least two of three blind trials. No universal numeric threshold is claimed.
- **Cancel:** target/sum mismatch, hidden configuration change, hallucinated content, leakage, phase/attack damage, or objective/listening disagreement.
- **Blockers:** the October paper's exact repository is empty; exact weights/config/environment/chunking are unavailable. The March CP-JKU code is not parity code.

This correction makes the future test genuinely multitrack-grounded and one-factor. It does not execute the experiment.
