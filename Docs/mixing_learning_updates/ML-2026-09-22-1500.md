The model operates on separately normalized overlapping `64 x 64` patches at 8 kHz and 256 ms. Thus the paper supports research on sparse measured RIR reconstruction, not a full-band music-reverb rule or a claim of perceptual superiority.

## Source Cards

### `SRC-ML-20260922-1500-01` — image-source barycenter

- Source: Yuyang Liu, Rumeshika Pallewela, Jesper Brunnström, Isabel Haasler and Filip Elvander, [Acoustic Image Source Interpolation with Optimal Transport Barycenter](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.15981v1), `arXiv:2609.15981v1`, 14 September 2026; arXiv perpetual non-exclusive licence.
- Reading depth: `full_text_complete` — signal model, grid and free-support formulations, initialization, all numerical experiments and limitations.
- Method: OT ground cost penalizes deviation from the physical-source displacement magnitude; a convex fixed-grid barycenter and a non-convex alternating free-support estimator are proposed.
- Reported experiment: 3 x 4 m two-dimensional shoebox; reflections up to second order; independent Gaussian point perturbations; 400 Monte Carlo trials in the main sweeps; default `sigma_x=0.1 m`, four known sources and four circular candidates per locus. One-PC circular initialization is the strongest practical initialization, but an oracle initialized from unperturbed targets remains better.
- Limits: equal reflection count and latent correspondence are assumed; simulation uses idealized point perturbations rather than measured detection errors; only image-source position error is evaluated; difficult centre/corner geometry sharply amplifies error; no RIR rendering, measured room, listening, localization, full-band, phase, peak, mono or music-production test.

### `SRC-ML-20260922-1500-02` — measured-RIR diffusion comparator

- Source: Sagi Della Torre, Mirco Pezzoli, Fabio Antonacci and Sharon Gannot, [On the Usefulness of Diffusion-Based Room Impulse Response Interpolation to Microphone Array Processing](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.28209v1), `arXiv:2603.28209v1`, 30 March 2026, CC BY 4.0; listed in the EUSIPCO 2026 programme.
- Reading depth: `full_text_complete`.
- Method: DiffusionRIR treats a time x microphone RIR matrix as an image, reconstructs missing microphone columns with diffusion/RePaint-style inpainting over overlapping `64 x 64` patches, separately normalizes patches to `[-1,1]`, restores scale and reassembles them.
- Simulated task result: on a 16-microphone, 8 kHz, 256 ms Pyroomacoustics speech setup, reconstructed RIRs generally improve MVDR SIR/SI-SDR/STOI over using measured channels alone, but the most one-sided mask retains a large SI-SDR deficit.
- Measured result: on MeshRIR S1-M3969, two sparse geometries outperform spatial-cubic interpolation in NMSE and cosine distance; one 70%-masked example reports similar EDC slopes (`T60 0.31 s` reconstruction versus `0.34 s` target).
- Limits: measured-room section does not run the downstream beamformer or a listening test; simulated and measured results are not independent model families; one room/subset; 8 kHz and 256 ms truncation; no repeated-seed uncertainty, patch-boundary audit, full-band HF, phase/mono, peak, localization, long-tail or music-production evaluation.
