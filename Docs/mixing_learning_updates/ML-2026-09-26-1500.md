## Sources actually read

### `SRC-ML-20260926-1500-01` — SEND

- **Title:** *SEND: A Spatial Event Neural Detector for Intentional Object Motion in Immersive Music Mixing*
- **Primary:** <https://dafx.de/paper-archive/2026/papers/DAFx26_paper_09.pdf>
- **Official archive:** <https://www.dafx.de/paper-archive/details/T80_1TpdEwnMKbJFQZoZGg>
- **Stable ID:** `DAFx-26:paper_09`
- **Publication date:** `2026`
- **Discovery date:** `2026-09-26`
- **Depth:** full proceedings PDF, including data construction, labels, models, objective table, listening protocol and limitations

SEND is a framewise event detector, not a panner or trajectory generator. The source corpus contains 1,000 professional object-based immersive projects (about 260 hours). The target object is isolated, the static 7.1.2 bed is rendered binaurally, and other dynamic objects are muted. Audio is standardized to 48 kHz/24 bit and `-20 LUFS`; the split is 930/50/20 projects, training clips are 20 seconds and evaluation uses 10-second overlap. Movement labels come from thresholding consecutive 3-D coordinate displacement; about 30% of frames are active.

The reported test-set metrics are SEND F1 `0.7675`, AUC `0.8777`, MCC `0.7013` and event-IoU `0.6305`; the CRNN event-IoU is `0.4519`, and a DSP baseline `0.0495`. These numbers describe event-window agreement on the proprietary test set, not musical quality or trajectory accuracy.

The listening test used 20 experienced listeners, five excerpts and five conditions at a stated 85 dB SPL over headphones. Crucially, professional engineers authored the trajectories for both the SEND- and CRNN-constrained conditions; only the permitted windows came from the detector. SEND scored `4.00` for stability and `4.07` for musical appropriateness versus `4.13` and `4.40` for the unrestricted human reference. The paper reports `p<.01`, but the visible protocol does not fully specify the repeated-measures test, multiplicity handling or effect intervals. The correct claim is therefore: SEND supplied useful candidate windows for human-authored motion in this dataset, not autonomous human-level spatial mixing.

Only 20 projects form the objective test set; the dataset, code, weights and split manifest are not public. Genre balance, room/playback transfer and conventional stereo-rock transfer are not established.

### `SRC-ML-20260926-1500-02` — PAEDB

- **Title:** *PAEDB: A Synthetic Primary-Ambient Dataset Generation Pipeline for Automatic Upmixing Using Deep Neural Networks*
- **Primary:** <https://dafx.de/paper-archive/2026/papers/DAFx26_paper_14.pdf>
- **Stable ID:** `DAFx-26:paper_14`
- **Publication date:** `2026`
- **Discovery date:** `2026-09-26`
- **Depth:** full proceedings PDF, including dataset construction, models, objective and listening protocols, tables and limitations

PAEDB combines MUSDB18-HQ and MoisesDB. After filtering, 385 of 390 songs remain. Mixtures and four stems are treated as separate inputs, producing 1,925 base entries and, after decomposition, 1,809 usable triplets or 5,427 files—about 550 hours and 150 GB. The paper restricts the dataset to non-commercial research use.

The targets are synthetic: centre-channel extraction produces a primary estimate, then ELAE estimates ambience. A fixed 12-ms surround delay and fixed fifth-order filters are used; LFE is low-passed at 200 Hz and L/C/R are high-passed at 150 Hz. These values define one target generator and are not mix presets. A Mel-RoFormer and a three-layer MLP were trained on two A10 GPUs. Mel-RoFormer reached `2.22±2.21 dB` SDR, `3.44±1.61 dB` SI-SDR and `106.93±10.80` LSD; the MLP was worse. Because ELAE generated the target, these results measure imitation of ELAE, not recovery of real recorded ambience.

The listening protocol reports 12 participants, six one-minute excerpts, four systems and three seven-point criteria. That design implies 864 participant-excerpt-system-criterion ratings, but the paper reports 264 observations, without a clear exclusion or aggregation rule. Friedman `p` values establish a system-level difference only; corrected pairwise results are not reported, so the data do not prove Mel-RoFormer equals ELAE or beats every baseline. Test media were extracted from YouTube at 128 kb/s and are not acceptable project reference assets; none was downloaded.

### `ART-ML-20260926-1500-02` — PAEDB repository

- **Repository:** <https://github.com/nick7ong/paedb>
- **Pinned commit:** [`d9b1386`](https://github.com/nick7ong/paedb/commit/d9b1386b2adb9a156d2884e40a5ad907cb13d547)
- **Licence:** MIT for repository code; source-audio rights remain separate and non-commercial/research constrained
- **Depth:** static README, dependency and generator-code inspection; nothing executed

The repository provides preprocessing/generation scripts but no trained weights, training/evaluation code, listener data, released manifests, checksums or audio examples. Most dependencies are unpinned and no environment lock is present.

`generate_paedb.py` recursively collects individual WAVs and shuffles them into train/validation/test with seed 42. Because mixture and stem WAVs from one song are separate files, a file-level split can leak the same musical performance across partitions. A group-by-song manifest is required before any model result is accepted.

The Torch ELAE path adds unseeded small random noise. It saves the mixture, computes `primary = input - ambience`, then independently fades the first 128 samples of primary and ambience without applying the same operation to the saved mixture. Exact additivity therefore fails at the start of each file even before quantization. A raw null is mandatory. Hugging Face cards mentioned outside the official paper were not verified because the permitted local `hf` client was unavailable; no Hub artifact is claimed accessible.

## Revised conclusions

- **Corrected:** SEND proposes movement windows; it does not generate the trajectories judged in the listening test.
- **Narrowed:** project-disjoint splitting is positive, but a proprietary 20-project test set and missing artifacts block independent validation.
- **Corrected:** PAEDB ambience is a CCD+ELAE target, not real room/studio ambience ground truth.
- **New risk:** PAEDB's public file-level random split can leak song identity across train/validation/test.
- **New defect:** separate start fades break the additive contract `mixture = primary + ambience` for the first 128 samples.
- **Statistics:** the PAEDB observation count is inconsistent with the described factorial design; Friedman significance alone is not pairwise superiority.
- **Rights:** YouTube-extracted evaluation media are excluded from the project; the MIT code licence does not grant rights to source audio.

## Knowledge cards

1. Event-window detection and spatial-trajectory generation are separate tasks and must be evaluated separately.
2. Human-authored trajectories inside model-selected windows confound attribution of listening scores to the detector.
3. Project-level splits reduce leakage but do not substitute for public manifests and broader held-out domains.
4. Event F1/IoU measures boundary agreement, not spatial stability, musicality or mono compatibility.
5. Broad `p<.01` claims without a complete repeated-measures and multiplicity protocol remain limited evidence.
6. Algorithmic primary/ambient targets train a processor emulator, not recovery of physical ambience.
7. Treating mixture and stems as independent files requires group-by-song splitting to prevent identity leakage.
8. Any preprocessing applied independently to additive components must also preserve or recompute the mixture.
9. An additive null must be measured before using generated primary/ambient pairs for training or metrics.
10. A Friedman omnibus test establishes a condition effect, not which pair differs.
11. Observation-count mismatches require unit-of-analysis and exclusion reconciliation before confidence claims.
12. A repository code licence does not override source-audio, dataset or extracted-media rights.

### `EXP-ML-20260926-1500-01` — SEND window-source A/B

- **Baseline A:** human-marked movement windows driving a fixed, preregistered subtle trajectory.
- **Variant B:** SEND-predicted windows driving the identical trajectory; window source is the only changed factor.
- **Controls:** same song, object, trajectory shape/range, render path, seed and gain. Preserve raw renders for peaks, phase and mono; use separate BS.1770-matched randomized copies for blind listening.
- **Measure:** onset/offset precision, event-IoU, movement duty cycle, boundary jitter, centre drift, mono residual, true peak and blind stability/musicality.
- **Protect:** centre anchor, section roles, transients, groove, width and mono compatibility.
- **Success:** predicted windows match or improve preference without higher jitter or safety regression.
- **Cancel:** detector timing causes chatter, centre instability, masking or no robust preference.
- **Blocker:** public SEND code, weights and test manifest were not found.

### `EXP-ML-20260926-1500-02` — PAEDB split-leakage audit

- **Baseline A:** repository file-level random split.
- **Variant B:** group-by-source-song split; split policy is the only changed factor.
- **Controls:** identical rights-cleared inputs, generated targets, model, budget, seed set and checkpoint rule.
- **Measure:** cross-partition song overlap, SDR/SI-SDR/LSD by song, confidence by held-out song and blind primary/ambient quality on separate loudness-matched copies.
- **Protect:** generalisation claim and source identity.
- **Success:** zero song overlap and stable performance on truly unseen songs.
- **Cancel:** reported advantage collapses under grouped splitting.

### `EXP-ML-20260926-1500-03` — additive-contract repair

- **Baseline A:** published path with separate 128-sample fades after decomposition.
- **Variant B:** one identical input-domain fade before decomposition; fade topology is the only changed factor.
- **Controls:** same audio, seed, decomposition, precision and file format.
- **Measure:** samplewise null, residual RMS/max, onset envelope, peak and phase.
- **Protect:** attacks and exact reconstruction.
- **Success:** residual is bounded by declared numeric/quantisation tolerance without new onset damage.
- **Cancel:** additivity remains broken or attack loss increases.

### `EXP-ML-20260926-1500-04` — synthetic mimic versus target generator

- **Baseline A:** ELAE primary/ambient target through a fixed 5.1 renderer.
- **Variant B:** learned Mel-RoFormer output through the identical renderer; extractor is the only changed factor.
- **Controls:** same licensed song, render gains/delay/filters and playback. Preserve raw renders; create separate BS.1770-matched blind copies.
- **Measure:** additive null, direct/ambient ratio, leakage, attacks, phase/correlation, peaks and blind envelopment/clarity/naturalness.
- **Protect:** centre, vocals/leads, punch, low end, room naturalness and headroom.
- **Success:** learned output matches or improves perception without leakage or safety regression on real held-out material.
- **Cancel:** preference is loudness-driven, only imitates synthetic quirks or damages direct sound.
