## Outcome

The two sources from the 09:00 search were re-read at protocol level and checked against two primary comparators. The main revisions are safety-relevant:

1. RESTORE's exact mixture-consistency constraint covers the five band-limited stems, not its generated HFE stem. Isolating HFE makes it auditable, but does not make it faithful.

## Sources actually read

### `SRC-ML-20260925-0900-01` — RESTORE, deep-review update

- **Primary:** <https://arxiv.org/html/2609.28683>
- **Stable ID:** `arXiv:2609.28683v1`
- **Publication date:** 2026-09-23
- **Depth:** full HTML re-read, including loss definitions, data construction, baseline parity, tables, runtime and limitations

RESTORE predicts vocals, music, broadband hiss, transient noise, residual and HFE. Its mixture-consistency loss requires only the first five, band-limited stems to reconstruct the degraded input; HFE is explicitly outside that constraint. The earlier conclusion is therefore narrowed: exact additivity limits deletion/reassignment in the existing band, but does not constrain invented high-frequency content.

Training uses 6-second synthetic mixtures built from five named music datasets. Ten MUSDB songs are held out for validation, but the paper does not publish a corpus-, song- and degradation-disjoint manifest for the 30-item paired test. Point estimates are reported without confidence intervals, significance tests or per-item results. Those data do not support the paper's broad “universal generalization” wording.

The steerability score is Spearman correlation between a scalar stem-gain sweep and the realized RMS energy. A fixed estimate multiplied by a scalar is expected to be monotonic, so a high score does not establish semantic independence. Likewise, “music bleed” is a projection-energy ratio, not an audible preservation test.

Historic FAD uses VGGish and CLAP embeddings, but the reference distribution is not identified. HFE is mixed: the instrumental CLAP-FAD worsens while VGGish-FAD improves. No listening test is reported. The approximately 50-times-real-time result is an A100 measurement, whereas the iZotope baseline is a rough GUI timing on an M2; that is not deployment-parity evidence.

**Revised conclusion:** treat the five-stem additive path and the unconstrained HFE path as separate evidence. Keep HFE off by default and require paired full-band, listener, transient, stereo/mono and peak validation.

### `SRC-ML-20260925-0900-02` — SSE, deep-review update

- **Primary:** <https://arxiv.org/html/2609.29169>
- **Stable ID:** `arXiv:2609.29169v1`
- **Publication date:** 2026-09-24
- **Depth:** full HTML re-read, including codec skips, flow target, dataset, four-step inference, ablations, objective tables and subjective protocol

SkipDACVAE adds learned encoder-to-decoder skip connections from the degraded input while the shared codec layers remain frozen. That path may preserve events, but it can also carry noise, reverberation or other degradations around the generative bottleneck. The large metric gains in the codec ablation do not isolate those two explanations.

SSE's flow target contains a target latent and a residual latent, but the paper states no latent- or waveform-sum constraint requiring their decoded sum to reconstruct the input. “Target plus residual” is therefore an output representation, not an additivity guarantee.

MuddyMix and DegradedMix use separator-derived pseudo-stems; instructions are generated from the undegraded target with Qwen2-Audio. The four Euler steps and other reported choices are selected “in our testing,” but no separate tuning split is described. On the single-ground-truth MuddyMix reconstruction table, SSE is worse than VisAH-FM on the reported envelope, magnitude and Wasserstein measures; the paper interprets this as creative multiplicity, which does not prove fidelity.

The subjective comparison is task-specific evidence for 10-second UGC clips. Trial accounting, loudness control, screening, blinding and multiple-comparison handling are insufficiently documented for transfer to music preservation.

**Revised conclusion:** SSE remains a generative UGC/movie system. Before any music use, test skip-path leakage, input conservation, no-generation baselines, prompt provenance and a complete loudness-matched listening protocol.

### `SRC-ML-20260925-1200-01` — FAD metric comparator

- **Title:** *Adapting Frechet Audio Distance for Generative Music Evaluation*
- **Primary:** <https://arxiv.org/html/2311.01616>
- **Stable ID:** `arXiv:2311.01616v2`
- **Publication date:** 2024-03-05
- **Discovery date:** 2026-09-25
- **Depth:** full HTML text, including sample-size study, embeddings, reference-set bias and listening correlation

This independent metric study shows that FAD depends materially on sample count, embedding and reference distribution, and proposes an asymptotic `FAD_inf` estimator. Reference-set genre/instrument bias can dominate comparisons, and VGGish-FAD correlates poorly with the reported audio- and musical-quality listening scores. It directly limits interpretation of RESTORE's small, undisclosed-reference historic FAD results.

**Bounded conclusion:** record the exact reference set, embedding/version, preprocessing, item/frame count and uncertainty. Do not compare FAD across studies without protocol parity, and do not treat it as preference or restoration fidelity.

### `SRC-ML-20260925-1200-02` — BABE-2 primary comparator

- **Title:** *A Diffusion-based generative equalizer for music restoration*
- **Primary:** <https://arxiv.org/html/2403.18636>
- **Stable ID:** `arXiv:2403.18636v2`
- **Publication date:** 2025-03-13 (revision)
- **Discovery date:** 2026-09-25
- **Depth:** full HTML text, including training, singer adaptation, historical evaluation, metrics and limitations
- **Artifact:** <https://github.com/eloimoliner/BABE2-music-restoration>

BABE-2 explicitly describes its output as a hybrid of original signal and learned prior, with reconstruction/hallucination dependent on the training data. Its historical FAD protocol names the MAESTRO reference set and multiple embeddings. For singing, it can fine-tune per singer; RESTORE's comparison uses generic BABE-2 checkpoints and omits that adaptation. RESTORE also does not provide a matched full-band HFE comparison against BABE-2.

The public BABE-2 repository has an MIT license, inference instructions, configurations and checkpoint links. It was inspected statically only; no code, checkpoint or audio was run.

**Bounded conclusion:** BABE-2 is a useful generative-restoration comparator, but baseline parity requires the same full-band target, adaptation policy, reference protocol and output safety checks.

### `EXP-ML-20260925-1200-01` — constrained sum versus HFE

- **Baseline A:** sum of the five mixture-consistent non-HFE RESTORE stems.
- **Variant B:** the identical sum plus HFE at gain `1`.
- **Only changed factor:** HFE contribution (`0` versus `1`).
- **Controls:** rights-cleared full-band rock excerpts degraded by preregistered low-pass cutoffs; same model, seed, stems, render and raw gain. Retain raw safety outputs; create separate BS.1770 loudness-matched blind copies.
- **Measure:** above-cutoff spectral error against clean target, harmonic/event precision, onsets and transient envelope, sample/true peak, stereo correlation, mono residual, artefact count and blind preference.
- **Protect:** drum attacks, guitar/bass identity, sibilance, stereo image, mono and headroom.
- **Success:** HFE improves bandwidth/preference without invented events, transient damage, image/mono failure or peak violation.
- **Cancel:** hallucination, brittle HF, correlated errors or loudness explains the preference.

### `EXP-ML-20260925-1200-03` — SSE skip-path leakage

- **Baseline A:** shared DACVAE reconstruction without degraded-input skips.
- **Variant B:** SkipDACVAE reconstruction with the same latent input.
- **Only changed factor:** skip connections enabled.
- **Controls:** identical rights-cleared clean/degraded pairs, codec weights, sample rate and output gain; no SSE flow generation.
- **Measure:** clean-target residual, retained noise/reverb, onset/transient error, phase/mono, sample/true peak and loudness-matched blind preference.
- **Protect:** wanted events, timing, attacks and ambience intent.
- **Success:** preservation improves without a material rise in degradation leakage.
- **Cancel:** skip path bypasses cleanup or adds peak/phase failure.

### `EXP-ML-20260925-1200-04` — SSE target/residual conservation

- **Variant B:** decoded SSE target plus decoded residual re-summed.

- **Controls:** fixed clip, video, prompt, checkpoint, seed, inference steps and raw gain; raw outputs plus separate loudness-matched copies.
