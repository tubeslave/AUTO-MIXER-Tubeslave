# Mixing Director v1.5: Vocal Director

Accepted baseline: v1.4 Drum Director.

Goals:
- keep the lead vocal anchored without making it flat;
- de-ess only actual high-frequency sibilant excess;
- add subtle level-matched saturation for density, not loudness;
- keep the secondary vocal distinct; do not treat it as a double unless evidence says so;
- allow the secondary vocal to support dense sections slightly more;
- retain the v1.3 shared room and all accepted drum reinforcement;
- no broad tonal EQ or long vocal hall yet.

Processing:
1. slow bounded vocal ride after the general Dynamics Director;
2. dynamic de-essing in the 4.8–10.5 kHz region, max 3 dB;
3. subtle level-matched saturation;
4. secondary vocal gets a small density-aware ride, max about +0.55 dB;
5. Vocal Critic rejects over-flattening, excessive de-essing, level cheating or broad tonal shift;
6. level-matched A/B against v1.4 is mandatory.
