# Mixing Director v1.2: Masking / EQ Director

Accepted baseline: v1.1 Dynamics.

The next stage solves relationships, not "pretty EQ" on isolated tracks.

Priority relationships:
- vocal over guitar/keys/playback in 1.2–3.5 kHz;
- vocal over cymbal wash in 3–6.5 kHz;
- kick transient/weight over bass only in 45–110 Hz;
- snare over guitar in presence when evidence supports it.

Method:
1. measure co-activity and band-energy competition;
2. propose only evidence-backed pairwise interventions;
3. use dynamic band cuts on the masker, keyed by target activity;
4. hard ceilings are 1.2–1.8 dB, so EQ cannot redesign the source;
5. do not boost the target if a smaller masker cut solves the conflict;
6. do not EQ inactive/non-conflicting pairs;
7. preserve v1.1 faders, pan, section automation and dynamics exactly;
8. no saturation, reverb, limiting or reference matching yet;
9. Masking Critic rejects worse separation or excessive mix tonal shift;
10. level-matched human A/B remains final acceptance.
