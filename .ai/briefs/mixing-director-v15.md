# Mixing Director v1.5 Automation
Automation is allowed to solve problems that static processing should not.
- slow lead-vocal rides, max +/-1.5 dB;
- shallow music-under-vocal duck, max 0.7 dB;
- backing vocal may rise modestly in dense sections;
- section energy should come from width/density/foreground relationships, not a large master-level jump;
- FX throws require phrase-end evidence and are not mandatory.
All rides are smoothed and must remain musically slower than individual waveform cycles.
