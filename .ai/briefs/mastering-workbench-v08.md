# Mastering Workbench v0.8: Section-aware Director

Quality baseline: v0.7 Compound.

The director does not identify verse/chorus by name. It infers a slowly varying density curve from
short-term energy and crest behavior, then makes only small parameter deviations around v0.7.

Low density:
- slightly less Clarity
- less Impact
- less clip drive
- less Maximizer drive

High density:
- slightly more control, still bounded

Automation is smoothed on a long time scale so mastering does not chase individual hits.
The section-aware candidate must be rendered from the premaster and compared level-matched against
v0.7 with the Musical Regression Critic. It is not promoted unless the human listener prefers it.
