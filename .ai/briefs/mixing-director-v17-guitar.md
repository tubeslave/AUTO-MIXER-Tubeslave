# Mixing Director v1.7: Guitar Director

Accepted baseline: v1.6 Bass Director.

Goal: guitars form a powerful wall in dense sections without becoming the loudness jump or masking the vocal.

- preserve v1.2 vocal/snare masking cuts;
- dynamically tame only low-mid buildup around 140–420 Hz;
- retain brightness; do not broadly cut high mids;
- original guitar remains the positional anchor;
- add a low-level decorrelated wall layer only as arrangement density rises;
- the wall layer is band-limited and delayed differently L/R, not a loud duplicate;
- no extra wall in sparse sections;
- Guitar Critic rejects level cheating, excessive side energy, thinning or presence harshness;
- level-matched A/B against v1.6 is mandatory.
