# Mixing Director v1.4 Instrument Directors
Vocal: bounded de-essing after editing/compression; no heavy wet FX by default.
Bass: optional shallow kick-triggered ducking, max 1.2 dB, to improve low-end separation without audible pumping.
Drums: sample reinforcement is evidence-only, never automatic by genre.
Guitar: do not fabricate a double; use recorded arrangement and existing stereo playback for width.
All instrument-director actions must be individually bypassable and regression-tested.
