# Mixing Director v1.8: Keys + Playback Director

Inserted before the autonomous iteration loop because this song contains both stereo Keys and stereo Playback.

Analysis of this multitrack:
- Keys are comparatively quiet and low-mid weighted;
- Playback is much stronger, wider and functions as a substantial arrangement bed.

Rules:
1. keep the accepted v1.7 mix as baseline;
2. preserve existing vocal masking from v1.2;
3. Keys: control only episodic 180–500 Hz buildup, max 1.2 dB; do not turn them into a foreground instrument;
4. Keys may gain a small amount of density-dependent stereo support, but no broadband level jump;
5. Playback: use a very slow bounded section ride, max +/-0.6 dB, to stop the arrangement bed moving the balance around;
6. Playback low-mid control is gentler, max 0.9 dB;
7. preserve the already-wide Playback image; do not add a generic widener;
8. no static smile EQ, no extra sub boost, no long reverb;
9. Critic rejects loudness cheating, low-mid hollowing, excess width and over-flattening;
10. after acceptance, Autonomous Iteration Loop becomes v1.9 and operates on the complete instrument-aware baseline.
