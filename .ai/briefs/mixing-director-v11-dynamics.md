# Mixing Director v1.1: Dynamics Director

Human evaluation of the fader/pan baseline was positive, with one clear weakness: individual sources
have enough performance dynamics that the balance moves as instruments rise and fall.

Therefore Dynamics Director is inserted before Masking/EQ for this experiment.

Principles:
- preserve the accepted v1.0 static balance and section automation;
- compress only tracks whose active short-term spread demonstrates a need;
- role-specific attack/release and GR ceilings;
- no automatic makeup gain: Balance Director remains authoritative;
- vocals and bass may receive stronger stability control;
- guitars moderate control;
- drums retain attack with slower/bounded compression;
- overheads/cymbals are nearly untouched;
- compare source stability and full-mix level-matched A/B;
- reject if a source becomes over-flat or drum transient behavior is audibly damaged.

This stage solves level instability only. EQ, masking, space, saturation and limiting remain disabled.
